A trip to the mountains

Hi Lena! Last weekend I went on a trip to the mountains with my friends. We got the train on Friday evening and got there at midnight.

On Saturday we went for a long walk. Then it started to rain and we got very wet! In the afternoon my friend Max went fishing, and I went swimming in the lake.

In the evening we had a party. I made a cake, and Max did the cooking. We had a great time.

On Sunday I had a headache, so I had a rest. We got home late. Now I have to do the washing and make a shopping list!
