musicмузыка is an uncountable noun. We do not use it in the plural.

The band played fantastic music [NOT fantastic musics].

musicalмузыкальный is an adjective.

There is a shop on King Street that sells musical instrumentsинструменты [NOT music instruments].

A musicianмузыкант (noun) is a person.

My brother is a very good musician. He plays three instruments.

Error warning
A band means a group of musicians. We do not say 'a music band'. Nuala had a really good band at her 21st birthday party.
A concert means an event with music. We do not say a 'music concert'. We're going to a concert tonight.

Слова: bandгруппа , concertконцерт .
