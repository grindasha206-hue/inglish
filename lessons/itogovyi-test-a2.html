My trip to London

My name is Daria. I'm from Oryol, and I've been learning English for a year. When I started, I couldn't say anything, but now I can talk about my life.

Last summer I went to London for two weeks. I stayed with a family who lived near the river. The flat they had was small, but they were very kind to me.

Every morning I got up at seven, had breakfast and took the bus to my language school. The lessons started at nine and finished at one.

In the afternoons I walked round the city. London is much bigger than Oryol, and it's more expensive, but it isn't as quiet. I visited a lot of museums because I'm interested in history.

One day it was raining, so I stayed at home and watched films in English. I didn't understand everything, but I understood more than I expected.

If I had more money, I would go back next year. I'm going to work hard and save money. When I go to London again, I'll speak English much better!
