Have you ever…? (present perfect 3)

Опыт за всю жизнь: ever, never, gone и been

A
Presentation

Опыт за всю жизнь

Говорим об опыте за всю жизнь и сравниваем gone и been.

JAMES
Have you been to Rome?
Yes, I have. Many times.
Have you ever been to Japan?
No, I've never been to Japan.
ANNA

A · время от прошлого до сейчас

Present perfect (have been / have had / have played…) используем, когда говорим о времени от прошлого до настоящего — например, о жизни человека.

Have you ever been to Japan?

time from the past until now

  • 'Have you been to Italy?' — 'No, I haven't.'
  • I've been to Spain, but I haven't been to Portugal.
  • Olga is an interesting person. She has had many different jobs and has lived in many places.
  • I've seen that man before, but I can't remember where.
  • How many times has our team won the cup?
  • 'Have you read this book?' — 'Yes, I've read it twice.' (twice = two times)

B · present perfect + ever (в вопросах) и never

  • 'Has Max ever been to Japan?' — 'Yes, once.' (once = one time)
  • 'Have you ever played tennis?' — 'Yes, I play a lot.'
  • My brother has never travelled by train.
  • I've never ridden a camel.
  • 'Who is that woman?' — 'I don't know. I've never seen her before.'

C · gone и been

Ben has gone to Spain.
(= he is in Spain now)

Ben has been to Spain.
(= he went to Spain, but now he is back)

Compare (сравните)

  • I can't find Olga. Where has she gone? (= where is she now?)
  • Oh, hello Olga! I was looking for you. Where have you been?

Reading

Grandma Vera's life

Что бабушка Вера уже делала в жизни, а чего — никогда? Найдите ever, never и been.

My grandmother Vera is seventy-five, and she has had a very interesting life.

She has lived in three different countries, and she has been to more than thirty. She has seen a volcano in Iceland and she has ridden a camel in the desert.

She has never flown in a helicopter, but she wants to try. 'I've never been to Japan either,' she says. 'Perhaps next year!'

And me? I've been abroad only twice. Grandma laughs: 'You're young. You haven't seen the world yet!'
