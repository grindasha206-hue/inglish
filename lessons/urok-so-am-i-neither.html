too и either ставятся в конце предложения.

We use too after a positive verb:

  • A: I'm happy.
    B: I'm happy too.
  • A: I enjoyed the film.
    B: I enjoyed it too.
  • Jane is a doctor. Her husband is a doctor too.

We use either after a negative verb:

  • A: I'm not happy.
    B: I'm not happy either.
  • A: I can't cook.
    B: I can't either.
  • Ben doesn't watch TV. He doesn't read newspapers either.

so am I = I am too
so have I = I have too (etc.):

  • A: I'm working.
    B: So am I. (= I'm working too)
  • A: I was late for work today.
    B: So was Sam.
  • A: I work in a bank.
    B: So do I.
  • A: We went to the cinema last night.
    B: Did you? So did we.
  • A: I'd like to go to Australia.
    B: So would I.

neither am I = I'm not either
neither can I = I can't either (etc.):

  • A: I haven't got a key.
    B: Neither have I. (= I haven't either)
  • A: Kate can't cook.
    B: Neither can Tom.
  • A: I won't (= will not) be here tomorrow.
    B: Neither will I.
  • A: I never go to the cinema.
    B: Neither do I.

Можно также Nor (= Neither):

  • A: I'm not married.
    B: Nor am I. or Neither am I.

Запомните: So am I (не So I am), Neither have I (не Neither I have).

Maria and I

Найдите все места с too, either, so и neither.

Maria and I met at English class, and we have a lot in common. Maria is learning English, and so am I. She likes cooking, and I like it too.

Maria doesn't like cold weather. Neither do I! We both want to live somewhere warm. She'd like to go to Australia, and so would I.

We aren't the same in everything. Maria can ride a bike, but I can't. I go to the cinema a lot, but Maria doesn't.

Maria has never run a marathon. Neither have I — and I don't want to either!
