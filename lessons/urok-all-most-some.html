A · Compare

children/money/books etc. (in general):

  • Children like playing.
    (= children in general)
  • Money isn't everything.
    (= money in general)
  • I enjoy reading books.
  • Everybody needs friends.

the children / the money / these books etc.:

  • Where are the children?
    (= our children)
  • I want to buy a car, but I don't have the money. (= the money for a car)
  • Have you read these books?
  • I often go out with my friends.

C · all of it / most of them / none of us etc.

  • You can have some of this cake, but not all of it.
  • A: Do you know those people?
    B: Most of them, but not all of them.
  • Some of us are going out tonight. Why don't you come with us?
  • I have a lot of books, but I haven't read any of them.
  • 'How many of these books have you read?' 'None of them.'

My English class

Найдите all, most, some, none и of.

There are thirty students in my English class. Most of them are women, and some of them are men. All of us work during the day.

Most people in the class want to travel. Some of the students have been to London, but none of them have been to Australia.

Our teacher gives us a lot of homework. I do most of it, but not all of it! Some exercises are very difficult.

Last week we had a test. All the students passed, but none of us got 100%. Most children love tests — but not adults!
