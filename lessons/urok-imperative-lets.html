Do this! Don't do that! Let's do this!

Просьбы, запреты и предложения сделать вместе

A
Presentation

Do this! Don't do that! Let's do this!

A · come / look / go / wait / be … — когда говорим кому-то что-то сделать

  • 'Come here and look at this!' — 'What is it?'
  • I don't want to talk to you. Go away!
  • I'm not ready yet. Please wait for me.
  • Please be quiet. I'm working.

also

  • Bye! Have a good holiday! / Have a nice time! / Have a good flight! / Have fun! (= I hope you have a good holiday etc.)
  • 'Have a biscuit.' — 'Oh, thanks.' (= would you like a biscuit?)

B · don't … — когда говорим кому-то НЕ делать

  • Be careful! Don't fall.
  • Please don't go. Stay here with me.
  • Be here on time. Don't be late.

C · Let's … — когда предлагаем сделать что-то вместе. Let's = Let us

  • It's a lovely day. Let's go out. (= you and I can go out)
  • Come on! Let's dance. (= you and I can dance)
  • Are you ready? Let's go.
  • Let's have pasta for dinner tonight.
  • A: Shall we go out tonight?
    B: No, I'm tired. Let's stay at home.

Отрицание — Let's not …:

  • It's cold. Let's not go out. Let's stay at home.
  • Let's not have pasta tonight. Let's have chicken.

Cooking class

Найдите все команды (wash, cut…), запреты (Don't…) и Let's.

Welcome to our cooking class! Today we're making a simple tomato soup. Listen carefully and follow the pan class="chunk">instructions.

First, wash your hands. Then cut two onions and four tomatoes. Be careful with the knife — don't cut your fingers!

Put some oil in the pan and add the onions. Don't put the heat too high. After five minutes, add the tomatoes and some water.

Now wait twenty minutes. Don't open the pan too often. OK, it's ready! Let's try it. Let's not add more salt — it's perfect. Have a nice lunch!
