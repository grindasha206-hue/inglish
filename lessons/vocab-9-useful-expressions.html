B · Expressions

Как предложить что-то

Нажмите 🔊 у реплики, чтобы послушать.

A: Why don't we …?Почему бы нам не go to the cinema this evening? (used to make a suggestion)
B: Good idea. Let's …Давай go and see a film and then have a meal.
A: OK! Which film do you want to see?
B: I don't mind.Мне всё равно. [It's all the same to me.] It's up to you.Решай сам. [You can decide.]
A: How about …?Как насчёт / What about …?Как насчёт the new Angelina Jolie film?
B: Great!
Error warning
Why don't we go … [NOT Why don't we going] or Let's go … [NOT Let's going].
How about going … [NOT How about go] or What about going … [NOT What about go].
B · Expressions

Как реагировать

A: I was late for work today.
B: Oh dear!Ой, беда! / Ну надо же! Was your boss angry? (surprised or disappointed)
A: I forgot to bring your book!
B: Oh, It doesn't matter.ничего страшного I don't need it.
A: I'm sorry, but I can't come to your party.
B: What a pity!Как жаль! (disappointed)
A: I passed my exam.
B: Well done!Молодец!
A: Hurry up!Поторопись! The taxi's here. [be quick]
A: Look out!Осторожно! / Be careful!Будь осторожен! – there's a car coming.
A: We need to buy Marta's birthday present.
B: Absolutely!Абсолютно! Конечно! What about getting her a CD? (agree strongly)
A: I agree.Я согласен. Let's go shopping this afternoon.
Error warning
I agree or I don't agree [NOT I am agree or I'm not agree].
