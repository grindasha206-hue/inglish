A · relative clauses 1

I can speak six languages.
I met a woman. She can speak six languages.
2 sentences

she → who

I met a woman who can speak six languages.
1 sentence
Jack was wearing a hat. It was too big for him.
2 sentences

it → that or which

Jack was wearing a hat that was too big for him.
or
Jack was wearing a hat which was too big for him.
1 sentence

B · who — для людей (не вещей)

A thief is a personwho steals things.
Do you know anybodywho can play the piano?
The manwho phoneddidn't give his name.
The peoplewho work in the officeare very friendly.

C · that — для вещей или людей

An airplane is a machinethat flies.
Emma lives in a housethat is 400 years old.
The peoplethat work in the officeare very friendly.

Для людей можно that, но who — чаще.

D · which — для вещей (не людей)

An airplane is a machinewhich flies. (не a machine who …)
Emma lives in a housewhich is 400 years old.

Не используйте which для людей:

  • Do you remember the woman who was playing the piano at the party? (не the woman which …)

Our street

Найдите who, which, that.

In our street there are a lot of people who do interesting jobs.

Mr Brown is a butcher — a person who sells meat. His wife is a dentist who takes care of people's teeth.

Their son is a musician who plays the guitar in a band, and their daughter has a car which is too small for her.

There is also an old house that is 400 years old. The woman who lives there is a genius — she can speak six languages!

Вопросы по прослушанному

Who was the man who phoned? I don't know. He didn't give his name. Was it the man who works in the office? No. It was someone who wants to buy our car. The car which is in the garage? Yes, the one that doesn't start!
