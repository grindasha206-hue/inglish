A
Presentation

It … — время, день, расстояние, погода

A · It — для time / day / distance / weather

time

  • What time is it?
  • It's half past ten.
  • It's late.
  • It's time to go home.

day

  • What day is it?
  • It's Thursday.
  • It's 16 March.
  • It was my birthday yesterday.

distance

our house
3 kilometres
city centre
  • It's three kilometres from our house to the city centre.
  • How far is it from Moscow to Saint Petersburg?
  • It's a long way from here to the airport.
  • We can walk home. It isn't far.

far — в вопросах (is it far?) и отрицаниях (it isn't far). В утверждениях — a long way (it's a long way).

weather

  • It's raining. It isn't raining. Is it snowing?
  • It rains a lot here. It didn't rain yesterday. Does it snow very often?
  • It's warm / hot / cold / fine / cloudy / windy / sunny / foggy / dark etc.
  • It's a nice day today.

Compare it и there

  • It snows a lot in winter.
    There is a lot of snow in winter.
  • It was very windy.
    There was a strong wind yesterday.

B · It's nice to … etc.

It's
easy / difficult / impossible / dangerous / safe /
expensive / interesting / nice / wonderful / terrible etc.
to …
  • It's nice to see you again.
  • It's impossible to understand him.
  • It wasn't easy to find your flat.

C · Не забывайте it

  • It's snowing again. (не Is snowing again)
  • Is it true that you're moving? (не Is true that…)

A letter from my new city

Найдите все предложения с It. О чём они: время, день, расстояние, погода?

Hi Lena! It's Sunday evening, and it's raining again. It rains a lot here in autumn.

It's 11 o'clock now, and it's dark outside. It was my birthday yesterday! It was sunny and warm, so we had a picnic in the park.

My new flat is great, but it's a long way from my office. It's about fifteen kilometres. It isn't easy to get there in the morning — it takes an hour by bus.

Is it true that you're coming in December? It would be wonderful to see you! It's cold here in winter, so bring a warm coat.

Вопросы по прослушанному

Excuse me, what time is it? It's half past four. Thanks. Is it far to the station? No, it isn't far. It's about five minutes on foot. Great. Is it going to rain? Maybe. It's very cloudy. It's a good idea to take an umbrella.
