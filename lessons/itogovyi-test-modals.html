Sounds & Speech

Модальные глаголы на слух

Слушайте и повторяйте. Обычная и медленная скорость помогают разобрать фразу.

1 · might / can / could

It might rain.
Could you open the door, please?

2 · must / have to

You must see it.
I have to go to the dentist.

3 · should / would

You should go to bed.
Would you like some tea?

4 · let's / used to

Let's go!
I used to live in a village.

Вопросы по прослушанному

Excuse me, could you help me? I'd like a ticket to Oxford. Of course. Would you like a single or a return? A return, please. Do I have to change trains? No, you don't need to change. But you should hurry — the train leaves in five minutes. Can I pay by card? Yes, you can. Have a good trip!
