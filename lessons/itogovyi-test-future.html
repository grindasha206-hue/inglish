Reading

Dasha's holiday

Next month Dasha is going to take her first holiday abroad. She's flying to Turkey on the 5th. Her plane leaves at 6.40 in the morning.

She's staying in a small hotel by the sea. She's going to swim every day and read a lot of books. 'I think I'll come back very brown!' she says.

Her friend Ira isn't going with her — she's working that week. 'Don't worry, I'll send you lots of photos,' Dasha promised.

'Will you bring me a present?' asked Ira. 'Of course! Shall I buy you some Turkish sweets?'
