Reading

Nina in Rome

Last summer Nina was on holiday in Italy. The weather was hot and the sea was warm.

On the third day she went to Rome by train. While she was walking near the Colosseum, she lost her bag.

She didn't have her phone or her money. A police officer helped her, and they found the bag in a café. A waiter was keeping it for her!

In the evening Nina had dinner in a small restaurant. She was very happy — it wasn't a bad day after all.

Sounds & Speech

Прошедшее время на слух

Слушайте и повторяйте. Обычная и медленная скорость помогают разобрать фразу.

1 · was /wəz/ и were /wə/

В середине фразы — коротко, без ударения; в кратком ответе — полно.

I was tired.
They were at home.
Yes, I was.
Yes, they were.

2 · Окончание -ed: /t/, /d/, /ɪd/

worked
cleaned
started

3 · Did you…? и didn't

В быстрой речи «did you» звучит как «диджу».

Did you see it?
What did you do?
I didn't know.

4 · was / were + -ing и when

I was reading when the phone rang.
What were you doing at ten?
