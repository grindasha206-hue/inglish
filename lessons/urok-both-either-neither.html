A · both / either / neither — о двух вещах или людях

both
?
?
either (or)
neither (not + either)
  • Rebecca has two children. Both are married. (both = the two children)
  • Would you like tea or coffee? You can have either. (either = tea or coffee)
  • A: Do you want to go to the cinema or the theatre?
    B: Neither. I want to stay at home. (neither = not the cinema or the theatre)

Compare either и neither:

'Would you like tea or coffee?''Either. I don't mind.' (= tea or coffee)
'I don't want either.' (не I don't want neither)
'Neither.' (= not tea or coffee)

My two brothers

Найдите both, either, neither.

I have two brothers, Max and Ivan. Both of them live in Moscow. Neither of them is married.

Last year I visited both of them. Max took me to a seafood restaurant, and Ivan took me to the theatre. I liked both evenings very much.

My brothers asked me: 'Moscow or Oryol — which city do you prefer?' I said: 'I like both cities. I don't want to choose either.'

Next summer we want to go on holiday together — to Spain or Italy. Either is fine for me. Neither of my brothers has been there.
