a/an …

Неопределённый артикль: a и an; another; профессии

A
Presentation

a/an …

A · a … = one thing or person

He's got a camera.
She's waiting for a taxi.
It's a beautiful day.
  • Rachel works in a bank. (не in bank)
  • Can I ask a question? (не ask question)
  • I don't have a job at the moment.
  • There's a woman at the bus stop.

B · an (не a) перед a/e/i/o/u

  • Do you want an apple or a banana?
  • I'm going to buy a hat and an umbrella.
  • There was an interesting programme on TV last night.

also an hour (h не произносится: an hour)
but a university (произносится yuniversity)
a European country (произносится yuropean)

another (= an + other) — одно слово:

  • Can I have another cup of coffee?

C · a/an — что это или кто это; профессии

a/an — когда говорим, что это за вещь или кто этот человек:

  • The sun is a star.
  • Football is a game.
  • Dallas is a city in Texas.
  • A mouse is an animal. It's a small animal.
  • Joe is a very nice person.
I'm a dentist.

a/an — для профессий и т.д.:

  • A: What's your job?
    B: I'm a dentist. (не I'm dentist)
  • 'What does Mark do?' 'He's an engineer.'
  • Would you like to be a teacher?
  • Beethoven was a composer.
  • Picasso was a famous painter.
  • Are you a student?
