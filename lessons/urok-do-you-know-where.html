A · Do you know where Paula is?

ANNA
Do you know where Paula is?
JAMES

We say: Where is Paula?
but Do you know where Paula is? (не Do you know where is Paula?)

Так же говорим:

I know
I don't know

B · Вопросы с do / does / did

Where does he live?
but Do you know where he lives? (не Do you know where does he live?)

C · Is …? / Do …? / Can …? etc. (yes/no questions)

Compare:

Is Jack at home?butDo you knowif / whether Jack is at home?
Have they got a car?if / whether they've got a car
Can Ben swim?I don't knowif / whether Ben can swim.
Do they live near here?if / whether they live near here
Did anybody see you?if / whether anybody saw you

В этих предложениях можно if или whether:

  • Do you know if they've got a car? or
    Do you know whether they've got a car?
  • I don't know if anybody saw me. or
    I don't know whether anybody saw me.

In a new town

Найдите все косвенные вопросы. Где стоит глагол?

Excuse me, can you help me? I'm new in this town. Do you know where the post office is? And can you tell me what time it opens?

I'm also looking for my friend Tom. I don't know where he lives. I don't remember what his street is called!

Do you know if there's a café near here? I'd like a coffee. And I don't know whether the museum is open on Mondays.

Oh, and one more question — do you know how much a taxi to the station is? Thank you very much!

Вопросы по прослушанному

Excuse me, do you know where the station is? Yes, it's on the left. Do you know what time your train leaves? I don't remember what time it is. Can you tell me what time it is now? It's half past three. Do you know if there's a bus to the station? I don't know whether it runs on Sundays, sorry.
