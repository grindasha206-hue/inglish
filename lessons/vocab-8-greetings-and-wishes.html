A · Every day

Прощание, просьбы, благодарность

When we leave someone we usually say Goodbyeдо свидания and also perhaps See you soon!до скорого! See you soon is quite informal.

When someone goes to bed, we usually say Goodnightспокойной ночи . We sometimes also say Sleep wellспи хорошо .

Don't say Goodnight when you arrive somewhere, only when you leave.

If you ask for something you usually say Pleaseпожалуйста (просьба) .

If someone does something nice for you, you say Thank youспасибо .
