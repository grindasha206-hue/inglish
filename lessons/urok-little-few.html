(a) little · (a) few

a little / a few = немного; little / few = почти нет

A
Presentation

(a) little · (a) few

A · (a) little + uncountable, (a) few + plural

(a) little + uncountable noun:

(a) little water
(a) little time
(a) little money
(a) little soup

a little water

(a) few + plural noun:

(a) few books
(a) few questions
(a) few people
(a) few days

a few books

B · a little = some but not much · a few = some but not many

a little = some but not much

  • She didn't eat anything, but she drank a little water.
  • I speak a little Spanish.
    (= some Spanish but not much)
  • A: Can you speak Spanish?
    B: A little.

a few = some but not many

  • Excuse me, I have to make a few phone calls.
  • We're going away for a few days.
  • I speak a few words of Spanish.
  • A: Are there any shops near here?
    B: Yes, a few.

C · little / few (без a) = почти нет

✗ little (без a) = nearly no or nearly nothing

  • There was little food in the fridge. It was nearly empty.

Можно very little:

  • Dan is very thin because he eats very little. (= nearly nothing)

✗ few (без a) = nearly no

  • There were few people in the theatre. It was nearly empty.

Можно very few:

  • Your English is very good. You make very few mistakes.

D · Compare little / a little и few / a few

  • They have a little money, so they're not poor. (= they have some money)
  • They have little money. They are very poor. (= nearly no money)
I have a little money. 🙂
I have little money. 🙁
  • I have a few friends, so I'm not lonely. (= I have some friends)
  • I'm sad and I'm lonely. I have few friends. (= nearly no friends)
I have a few friends. 🙂
I have few friends. 🙁

A quiet holiday

Найдите little, a little, few, a few.

Last week I went to a small village for a few days. There were very few people there — only a few old houses and a little shop.

The shop had little food, so I bought a little bread and a few eggs. I had a little money with me, so it was OK.

In the evenings there was little to do. I read a few books and wrote a few postcards. I speak a little English, so I talked to a tourist from London.

It was a good holiday. I needed a little rest. Now I'm back at work with a few new ideas!
