Learning to cook

Hi Sam! I've decided to learn to cook. My mum taught me to make soup last week, and I enjoyed cooking it a lot.

Yesterday I went to the market to buy vegetables. I had to wait for the market to open — I was too early! The seller let me try some cheese.

In the evening my friends came for dinner. They wanted me to make the soup again. My friend Olga suggested having a cooking party every Friday.

I'd love to take a cooking course. But I haven't got time to do it now. Let's cook together when you come!

Повторяем грамматику на слух

Слушайте и повторяйте. Обычная и медленная скорость помогают разобрать фразу.

1 · work / to work / working

Please be quiet. I'm working.

2 · to … / -ing

I enjoy dancing.
I'd love to go to Australia.

3 · want you to / make / let

I want you to be happy.
He makes me laugh.

4 · to / for

She went to the shop to get a newspaper.
They're waiting for the lights to change.
