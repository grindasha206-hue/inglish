D · Expressions

Выражения

A: Where can I find a mugкружка / a clothтряпка / some kitchen paper?
B: They are in the cupboard.
A: Can I help with the washing-upмытьё посуды / cooking?
B: Yes, please! You can dry the plates. / You can cook some rice.
A: Where does this cup / plate / frying pan go?
B: Put it in this cupboard, please.
Tip
Stick labels on objects in your kitchen with their English names on them. You will see these every day and this will help you to learn the words.

Exercise 11.1

Yes or no?

Неправильные утверждения из книги — исправьте.

Exercise 11.2

Make questions

Составьте вопрос к ответу. Образец: Where's the coffee? — It's in the cupboard.

Exercise 11.3

What do you need?

Образец: To make coffee I need a coffee maker, a cup, a spoon.

Exercise 11.4

Look at the picture

Ответьте по картинке. Образец: What's on the cooker? — a saucepan.
