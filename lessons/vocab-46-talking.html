We use say when we report someone's words. (sayсказать , saidсказал (прош. от say) )

She said, 'This is horrible!'

He said that he wanted a drink.

We say hello / goodbye and we say please / thank you / Happy Birthday / Merry Christmas / Happy New Year / Congratulations! (say helloпоздороваться , say goodbyeпопрощаться , say pleaseсказать «пожалуйста» )

We use say when we ask about language. (How do you say …?Как сказать …? )

B · Tell (tell / told / told)

tell

Tell is usually followed immediately by a person. Say is not followed immediately by a person. (tellсказать (кому-то), рассказать , toldсказал (прош. от tell) )

Tell is often used with how and wh-words (when, what, why, where) to find out and give information.

Tell me when you want to have dinner. She told me how to send a text message.

You can tell someone the time / a story / a joke / your name / address / phone number. (tell someone the timeсказать, который час , tell a storyрассказать историю , tell a jokeрассказать анекдот )

Error warning
He told me his name. [NOT He said me his name.]
Error warning
Can you tell me where the bus station is, please? [NOT Can you say me … ?]
C · Ask

ask

Ask is used for questions. (askспрашивать, просить )

My sister asked me where I was going. / My sister asked (me), 'Where are you going?'

A: Can I ask you a question? B: Yes. A: What day of the week were you born? B: Thursday. (ask a questionзадать вопрос )

You can ask someone the way / the time. (ask the wayспросить дорогу )

You can ask somebody to do something and ask someone for something. (ask somebody to doпопросить кого-то сделать , ask forпопросить (что-то) )

I asked him to turn off his radio. (or I said, 'Please turn off your radio.')

She asked for the bill. (or She said, 'Can I have the bill, please?')

D · Speak / talk / answer / reply

speak, talk, answer, reply

I like talking to you. [having a conversation with you] (talk toразговаривать с )

Error warning
Do you speak Japanese? (used for languages) [NOT Do you talk Japanese?] (speakговорить (на языке) )

Can you answer the telephone / the door, please? [pick up the phone / open the door to see who it is] (answerотвечать )

Teacher: Who can answer the next question? Joanna?

He didn't reply to my email. (also used for letters / faxes / texts) [he did not send me an email back] (reply toответить на )
