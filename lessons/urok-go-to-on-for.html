go to … · go on … · go for … · go -ing

Глагол go с to, on, for и -ing

A
Presentation

go to … · go on … · go for … · go -ing

A · go to …

go to … (go to work / go to London / go to a concert etc.)

  • What time do you usually go to work?
  • I'm going to China next week.
  • Sophie didn't want to go to the concert.
  • 'Where's Tom?' 'He's gone to bed.'
  • I went to the dentist yesterday.
go to

go to sleep = start to sleep:

  • I was very tired and went to sleep quickly.

go home (без to):

  • I'm going home now. (не going to home)

B · go on …

go onholiday
a trip
a tour
a cruise
strike
  • We're going on holiday next week.
  • Children often go on school trips.
  • Workers at the airport have gone on strike.
    (= they are refusing to work)

C · go for …

go (somewhere) fora walk
a run
a swim
a drink
a meal
  • 'Where's Emma?' 'She's gone for a walk.'
  • Do you go for a run every morning?
  • The water looks nice. I'm going for a swim.
  • I met Chris in town, so we went for coffee.
  • Shall we go out for a meal? I know a good restaurant.

D · go + -ing

go + -ing — для многих видов спорта (swimming / skiing etc.) и для shopping.

I go
he is going
we went
they have gone
she wants to go		shopping
swimming
fishing
sailing
skiing
jogging etc.
  • Are you going shopping this afternoon?
  • It's a nice day. Let's go swimming.
    (or Let's go for a swim.)
  • Richard has a small boat and he often goes sailing.
  • I went jogging before breakfast this morning.

Reading

Holiday in Greece

Найдите все сочетания с go.

Last summer we went on holiday to Greece. Every morning I went jogging on the beach, and my husband went for a swim.

One day we went on a boat trip to a small island. In the afternoon the children went fishing with their dad, and I went shopping in the market.

In the evening we often went out for a meal. The food was wonderful! After dinner we went for a walk and then went home.

On the last day the airport workers went on strike, so we couldn't go home! We went to the beach again. Nobody was sad!
