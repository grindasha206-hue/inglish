B · Expressions in reception

На ресепшене

Do you have a single roomодноместный номер [for one person] / a double roomдвухместный номер [for two people]?

I have a reservationбронь . [I booked a room] My name is …

We'd like a room with a viewномер с видом of the sea.

The receptionistадминистратор may say:

Here is your key.

Your room is on the first floor. Take the lift. It's over there. (take the liftподняться на лифте )

Would you like some help with your luggage?

Can you fill in this form, please? (fill in a formзаполнить бланк )

Sign (your name) here, please. (signрасписаться )

Please check your bill. [make sure it is correct] (check your billпроверить счёт )

When you leave you say:

Can I check outвыехать из отеля , please?

Can I have the bill, please?
