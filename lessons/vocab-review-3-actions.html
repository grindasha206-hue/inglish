A busy day at home

Повседневные действия — всё происходит прямо сейчас.

My father is cooking dinner in the kitchen. My mother is washing the car. My brother is doing his homework, and I'm reading a book.

Our dog isn't sitting quietly — it's running! Right now, it's raining, but we aren't watching TV.

C
Reading 2

What time is it?

Время и погода — в диалоге.

What's the time?
It's ten o'clock.
Is it sunny today?
No, it's raining, and it's very windy.
Are you going to work now?
Yes, I'm going now.
Are you late again?
No, I'm early today!
