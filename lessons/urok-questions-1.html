Questions 1: порядок слов в вопросах

A
Presentation

is it …? have you …? do they …? (questions 1)

A · Первый глагол — перед подлежащим

You are eating.
Are you eating?    What are you eating?

В вопросах первый глагол (is/are/have etc.) стоит перед подлежащим:

positive: subject + verbquestion: verb + subject
I am late.→Am I late?
That seat is free.→Is that seat free?
She was angry.→Why was she angry?
David has gone.→Where has David gone?
You have got a car.→Have you got a car?
They will be here soon.→When will they be here?
Paula can swim.→Can Paula swim?

Запомните: подлежащее стоит после первого глагола.

  • Where has David gone? (не Where has gone David?)
  • Are those people waiting for something? (не Are waiting …?)
  • When was the telephone invented? (не When was invented …?)

B · do …? / does …? / did …?

present simple questionsdoI/we/you/theywork/live/go etc. …?
doeshe/she/it
past simple questionsdidI/they/he/she etc.
positivequestion
They work hard.→Do they work hard?
You watch television.→How often do you watch television?
Chris works hard.→Does Chris work hard?
She gets up early.→What time does she get up?
They worked hard.→Did they work hard?
You had dinner.→What did you have for dinner?
She got up early.→What time did she get up?

Иногда do — основной глагол (do you do / did he do etc.):

  • What do you usually do at weekends?
  • A: What does your brother do?
    B: He works in a bank.
  • A: I broke my finger last week.
    B: How did you do that? (не How did you that?)

C · Why isn't …? / Why don't …? etc. (Why + negative)

  • Where's John? Why isn't he here? (не Why he isn't here?)
  • Why can't Paula come to the meeting tomorrow? (не Why Paula can't …?)
  • Why didn't you phone me last night?
