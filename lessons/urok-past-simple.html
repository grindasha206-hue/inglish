A
Presentation

Каждый вечер и вчера вечером

Картинка: пара смотрит телевизор. Они смотрят его каждый вечер (present simple) — и вчера вечером тоже смотрели (past simple).

present simpleThey watch TV every evening.
past simpleThey watched TV yesterday evening.

watched — это past simple

I / we / you / theywatched
he / she / itwatched

Форма одна для всех лиц.

Правильные глаголы (regular): + -ed

work → workeddance → danced
clean → cleanedstay → stayed
start → startedneed → needed

Правописание (spelling)

try → triedstudy → studiedcopy → copied
stop → stoppedplan → planned
  • I wash my hair every evening. Yesterday evening I washed my hair.
  • My mother worked in a school from 1998 to 2010.
  • Yesterday it snowed all day. It stopped in the evening.
  • We enjoyed the concert. We danced and talked to a lot of people. The concert finished at eleven.

Неправильные глаголы (irregular)

У неправильных глаголов past simple образуется не через -ed. Вот важные неправильные глаголы (полный список — в Словаре, вкладка «Неправильные глаголы»):

Reading

Max's bad day

Найдите в тексте глаголы в past simple — правильные и неправильные.

Last Saturday was a bad day for Max. He woke up late, so he missed the bus to the airport.

He took a taxi, but the traffic was terrible. When he finally arrived at the airport, he ran to the gate.

His plane left on time — without him! Max sat down and drank a coffee. Then he found his phone and called his grandfather.

His grandfather laughed and said: 'Don't worry. Come to my house tonight.' Max bought a ticket for the next flight and arrived in the evening. They had dinner together and talked until midnight.

Sounds & Speech

Три звука окончания -ed

Окончание -ed читается по-разному: /t/, /d/ или /ɪd/.

1 · /t/ после глухих: k, p, s, ʃ, tʃ

worked
stopped
danced

2 · /d/ после звонких и гласных

cleaned
stayed
played

3 · /ɪd/ после t и d — новый слог

started
needed
visited

4 · Во фразах

I watched TV and cleaned my room.
The film started at seven and ended at ten.

Мой вчерашний день

Напишите 6–8 предложений о том, что вы делали вчера или в прошлые выходные. Используйте и правильные, и неправильные глаголы.

  • In the morning I… — got up, had breakfast, went…
  • At work / at home I… — worked, cleaned, cooked…
  • In the evening I… — met, watched, read…
  • It was… — как прошёл день
0 слов
Пример: Yesterday I got up at seven and had a quick breakfast. I went to work by bus. I worked until six and answered a lot of emails. In the evening I met my friend in a café. We drank tea and talked for two hours. Then I went home, read a book and went to bed at eleven. It was a good day.
