Presentation

She said that … He told me that …

A · Что сказали друзья

Last week you went to a party. A lot of your friends were there. Here are some things they said to you:

Today you meet Paul. You tell him about the party. You tell Paul what your friends said:

CLARE
I'm enjoying my new job.
My father isn't well.
am
is → was
  • Clare said that she was enjoying her new job.
  • She said that her father wasn't well.
SARAH
We're going to buy a house.
BEN
are → were
  • Sarah and Ben said that they were going to buy a house.
PETER
I have to leave early.
My sister has gone to Australia.
have
has → had
  • Peter said that he had to leave early.
  • He said that his sister had gone to Australia.
KATE
I can't find a job.
can → could
  • Kate said that she couldn't find a job.
STEVE
I'll phone you.
will → would
  • Steve said that he would phone me.
RACHEL
I don't like my job.
My son doesn't like school.
do
does → did
  • Rachel said that she didn't like her job.
  • She said that her son didn't like school.
MIKE
You look tired.
I feel fine.
YOU
look → looked
feel → felt
etc. → etc.
(present) → (past)
  • Mike said that I looked tired.
  • I said that I felt fine.

B · say и tell

say (→ said)

  • He said that he was tired.
    (не He said me)
  • What did she say to you?
    (не say you)

Говорим he said to me, I said to Ann etc.
(не 'he said me', 'I said Ann').

tell (→ told)

  • He told me that he was tired.
    (не He told that)
  • What did she tell you?
    (не tell to you)

Говорим he told me, I told Ann etc.
(не 'he told to me', 'I told to Ann').

C · that можно не говорить

  • He said that he was tired. or He said he was tired. (без that)
  • Kate told me that she couldn't find a job. or Kate told me she couldn't find a job.

News from Olga

Найдите все предложения с said и told. Какие слова Ольга сказала на самом деле?

Yesterday I met my old friend Olga in town. She told me a lot of news. She said that she was living in Moscow now.

She said that she had a new job and that she was very happy there. She told me that her brother had got married last month.

I asked about her parents. Olga said that her mother wasn't well, but her father was fine. She said she couldn't visit them very often.

Before she left, Olga said that she would phone me next week. She told me I looked great! I said that I felt great too.
