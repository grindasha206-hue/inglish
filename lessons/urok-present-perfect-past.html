Не используем present perfect (have arrived / have done / have been etc.) с законченным временем:

  • I saw Olga yesterday. (не I have seen)
  • Where were you on Saturday evening? (не Where have you been)
  • We didn't go on holiday last summer. (не We haven't gone)
  • 'What did you do last night?' — 'I stayed at home.'
  • Pushkin lived from 1799 to 1837. He was a writer. He wrote many poems and stories.

Для вопросов When…? и What time…? — тоже past:

  • When did you buy your phone? (не When have you bought)
  • What time did Max leave? (не What time has Max left)

Grandpa Pavel

Где present perfect (вся жизнь до сейчас), а где past simple (законченное время)?

My grandfather Pavel has had an interesting life. He worked in a factory for thirty years, and then he became a taxi driver.

He has visited fifteen countries. He went to Cuba in 1985 and to India in 1999. Last year he flew to Japan — it was his first long flight.

He has written two books about his travels. He wrote the first one ten years ago, and he finished the second one last month.

Grandpa hasn't learned to use a computer yet, so he writes everything by hand. 'I've never needed a computer,' he says.
