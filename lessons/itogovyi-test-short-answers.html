My new flatmate

Hi Kate! I met my new flatmate, Anna, yesterday. We have a lot in common. I'm learning Spanish, and so is she! She likes cooking, and I do too.

But we're not the same in everything. Anna can drive, but I can't. I don't eat meat, but she does. She didn't like the flat at first, but I did.

Anna hasn't been to Spain. Neither have I — so we're going there together in May! You've been to Madrid, haven't you?

Don't forget to call me this weekend. I won't be at home on Saturday, but I will be on Sunday.
