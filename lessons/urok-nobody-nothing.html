not + anybody/anyone/anything · nobody/no-one/nothing

nobody, nothing, not anybody, not anything

A
Presentation

not + anybody/anyone/anything · nobody/no-one/nothing

A · Люди и вещи

not + anybody/anyone
nobody/no-one
(for people)

  • There isn't anybody (or anyone) in the room.
  • There is nobody (or no-one) in the room.
  • A: Who is in the room?
    B: Nobody. / No-one.
-body и -one — одно и то же:
anybody = anyone   nobody = no-one

not + anything
nothing
(for things)

  • There isn't anything in the bag.
  • There is nothing in the bag.
  • A: What's in the bag?
    B: Nothing.

B · not + any… = no…

not + anybody/anyone

  • I don't know anybody (or anyone) here.

nobody = not + anybody
no-one = not + anyone

  • I'm lonely. I have nobody to talk to.
    (= I don't have anybody)
  • The house is empty. There is no-one in it. (= There isn't anyone in it.)

not + anything

  • I can't remember anything.

nothing = not + anything

  • She said nothing.
    (= She didn't say anything.)
  • There's nothing to eat.
    (= There isn't anything to eat.)

C · В начале предложения или как ответ

nobody/no-one/nothing можно в начале предложения или отдельно (как ответ):

  • The house is empty. Nobody lives there.
  • 'Who did you speak to?' 'No-one.'
  • Nothing happened.
  • 'What did you say?' 'Nothing.'

D · Запомните

negative verb + anybody/anyone/anything
positive verb + nobody/no-one/nothing
  • He doesn't know anything. (не He doesn't know nothing)
  • Don't tell anybody. (не Don't tell nobody)
  • There is nothing to do in this town. (не There isn't nothing)

Reading

A surprise

Найдите anybody, nobody, anything, nothing.

Last night I came home late. The house was dark. 'Hello?' I said, but nobody answered. There wasn't anyone in the kitchen.

I went upstairs. Nothing was different, but I heard something. I didn't see anybody. I was scared!

Then the lights came on. 'Surprise!' All my friends were there — it was my birthday! I didn't know anything about the party.

'Did you tell anybody?' I asked my sister. 'No, nobody told you anything. That's why it's a surprise!'
