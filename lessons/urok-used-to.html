Отрицание — I didn't use to …:

  • When I was a child, I didn't use to like fish.

Вопрос — did you use to …?:

  • Where did you use to live before you came here?

C · used to — только для прошлого

Нельзя сказать «I use to …» о настоящем:

  • I used to play volleyball. These days I play tennis. (не I use to play tennis)
  • We usually get up early. (не We use to get up early)

Grandma's young days

Что бабушка делала раньше и что делает сейчас?

When my grandmother was young, life was very different. She used to live in a small village, and she used to walk five kilometres to school every day.

There was no TV in the village, so in the evenings people used to sing songs and tell stories. My grandmother used to play the guitar.

She didn't use to have a phone. When she wanted to talk to her friends, she used to write letters.

Now she lives in the city, has a smartphone and watches TV every evening. 'Life is easier now,' she says, 'but people used to talk to each other more.'
