C · Nature and conservation

Природа и её охрана

natureприрода means 'everything in the natural world'. (= animals, birds, plants, etc.) (conservationохрана природы /kɒnsəˈveɪʃn/)

Error warning
I love nature [NOT I love the nature]. I like walking in the countryside [NOT I like walking in the nature]. 'Nature' is not a place.

There is wonderful wildlifeдикая природа, животные in the north of the country. [animals, birds, fish and insects]

Near the village there is a conservation areaзаповедная зона . [place where wildlife and nature are protected]

In the south of the country, there is a national parkнациональный парк . [very big national conservation area]
