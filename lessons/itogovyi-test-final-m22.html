A Saturday morning

On Saturday morning I woke up early and got up at seven. I looked out of the window — it was sunny!

I put on my jacket and went out. The bus came and I got on. I got off in the centre and went into a shop.

I tried on a pair of shoes, but I didn't buy them. Then I went back home, took off my jacket and sat down.

I turned on the TV, but the programme was boring, so I turned it off and picked up a book.

2 · Ответьте или скажите о другом человеке

Вопросы на слух

Слушайте и повторяйте. Обычная и медленная скорость помогают разобрать фразу.

1 · in / out / on / off

2 · up / down / back

Would you like to sit down?
He'll be back on Monday.

3 · put it on

Here's your coat. Put it on.
Your shoes are dirty. Take them off.

4 · turn on / pick up

It was dark, so I turned on the light.
Can you pick them up for me?
