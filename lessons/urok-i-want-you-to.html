D · make и let

После make и let мы не используем to:

  • He's very funny. He makes me laugh. (не makes me to laugh)
  • At school our teacher made us work very hard.
  • I didn't have my phone with me, so Sue let me use hers. (не let me to use)

Можно сказать Let's … (= Let us), когда хотите, чтобы люди сделали что-то вместе с вами:

  • Come on! Let's dance.
  • 'Do you want to go out tonight?' 'No, I'm tired. Let's stay at home.'

My music story

Найдите конструкции want/ask/tell + somebody + to и make/let + somebody.

When I was a child, my parents wanted me to be a doctor. But I didn't want to. I wanted to be a singer!

My grandmother taught me to sing. She made me practise every day, but she let me choose the songs. My music teacher persuaded my parents to buy me a piano.

When I was eighteen, my father asked me to think again. My mother told me not to worry. 'Do what you love,' she said.

Now I'm a music teacher. I tell my students to enjoy music. I don't make them practise for hours. Let's sing together!
