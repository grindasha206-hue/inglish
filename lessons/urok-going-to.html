B · I am going to do something = я решил это сделать, это моё намерение

I decided to do it ——▶
I'm going to do it
past
now
future
  • I'm going to buy a new phone next month.
  • Max is going to sell his bike.
  • I'm not going to have lunch today. I'm not hungry.
  • What are you going to wear to the party?
  • 'The car is dirty.' — 'Yes, I know. I'm going to wash it.'
  • Are you going to invite Olga to your birthday?

Для будущего можно использовать и present continuous (I am doing), обычно для договорённостей (→ Unit 25):

  • I am meeting Anna tomorrow.

C · Something is going to happen

Something is going to happen = мы видим сейчас, что это точно случится:

  • Look at the sky! It's going to rain. (black clouds now → rain)
  • Oh no! It's 8.55 and I'm still at home. I'm going to be late. (8.55 now and still at home → late)

Reading

Nastya's plans

Что Настя решила сделать? Найдите все going to.

This year Nastya has made some big decisions. First, she's going to change her job. She's tired of her office, and she's going to work from home.

She's also going to learn to drive. She has already found a driving school, and the lessons are going to start next month.

And in the summer she's going to travel to Georgia with her sister. They aren't going to stay in a hotel — they're going to rent a small flat by the sea.

But today Nastya isn't going to do anything. Look at the sky — it's going to rain, and she's going to stay at home with a good book.

Sounds & Speech

going to на слух

Слушайте и повторяйте.

1 · going to /ˈɡəʊɪŋ tə/

В быстрой речи going to звучит как «gonna», но пишем всегда going to.

I'm going to watch TV this evening.
She's going to sell her car.

2 · Вопросы

What are you going to wear?
Are you going to invite Mark?

3 · Видно, что случится

Look at the sky! It's going to rain.
I'm going to be late.

Listening

Слушаем вслепую

Вопросы по прослушанному

What are you going to do after the lesson, Kate? I'm going to buy some food. My parents are coming for dinner. What are you going to cook? I'm going to make pasta. And you? I'm going to go to the gym. Take an umbrella — it's going to rain!
