You can also say: Naomi has (got) a red coat on.

You carry a bag and an umbrellaзонт .

In the morning you get dressedодеваться or put your clothes on. At night you get undressedраздеваться or take your clothes off.

Error warning
You put clothes on but you take clothes off [NOT put clothes off].
Tip
When you get dressed in the morning, say to yourself Now I'm putting on my socks. Now I'm putting on my shoes and so on.
