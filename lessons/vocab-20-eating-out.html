caféкафе : you can have a cup of tea / coffee and a snackперекус there (e.g. a sandwich or a cake). They sometimes serve mealsеда (e.g. lunch, dinner) too.

restaurantресторан : you go there for a meal; more expensive than a café.

barбар / pubпаб : bars and pubs serve alcoholалкоголь and soft drinksбезалкогольные напитки [non-alcoholic drinks, e.g. fruit juice]; you can usually have a meal or a snack there too.

fast food restaurantресторан быстрого питания : you can get a quick hot meal there, for example burger and chips.

take-awayеда навынос (заведение) : you buy a snack or a meal there and take it home to eat.
