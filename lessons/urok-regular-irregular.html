Правильные и неправильные глаголы

A · Regular verbs (правильные)

Past simple и past participle правильных глаголов оканчиваются на -ed:
clean → cleaned   live → lived   paint → painted   study → studied

Past simple (→ Unit 11)

  • I washed my car yesterday.
  • Olga studied medicine at university.

Past participle

have/has + past participle (present perfect → Units 15–18):

  • I have washed my car.
  • Max has lived in Moscow for five years.

be (is/are/were/has been etc.) + past participle (passive → Units 21–22):

  • These windows are cleaned every week.
  • My phone has been repaired.

B · Irregular verbs (неправильные)

Past simple и past participle неправильных глаголов не оканчиваются на -ed:

makebreakcut
past simplemadebrokecut
past participlemadebrokencut

  • I have made some tea. (past participle — present perfect)
  • Cheese is made from milk. (past participle — passive present)

Dima's first marathon

Найдите правильные (-ed) и неправильные глаголы. Какие формы — past simple, какие — past participle?

Last Sunday my brother Dima ran his first marathon. He began training a year ago, and he has run every morning since then.

The race started at nine. Dima ran quickly at the beginning, but in the middle of the race he felt tired. He drank some water and kept going.

He finished in four hours. He didn't win, of course, but he was really happy. 'I've never been so tired in my life!' he said.

After the race we went to a café. Dima ate two pizzas and slept for twelve hours. He has already chosen his next marathon!
