there is · there are

Что где находится и что есть

A
Presentation

there is / there are

A · there is / there are

There's a man on the roof.

There's a train at 10.30.

There are seven days in a week.

singular

there is …  (there's)
is there … ?
there is not …  (there isn't или there's not)
  • There's a big tree in the garden.
  • There's nothing on TV tonight.
  • A: Do you have any money?
    B: Yes, there's some in my bag.
  • A: Excuse me, is there a bank near here?
    B: Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
  • We can't go skiing. There isn't any snow.

plural

there are …
are there … ?
there are not …  (there aren't)
  • There are some big trees in the garden.
  • There are a lot of cars on this road.
  • A: Are there any cafés near here?
    B: Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
  • This café is very quiet. There aren't many people here.
  • How many players are there in a volleyball team?
  • There are 6 players in a volleyball team.

B · there is и it is

there is

There's a book on the table.
(не It's a book on the table.)

it is

I like this book. It's interesting.
(It = this book)

Compare

  • 'What's that noise?' — 'It's a plane.' (It = that noise)
  • There's a bus at 8.15. It's a fast bus. (It = the 8.15 bus)
  • There's a lot of sugar in this tea. I don't like this tea. It's too sweet. (It = this tea)

Reading

Green Village

Что есть в деревне, а чего нет? Найдите there is / there are.

Welcome to Green Village! It's a small place, but there are a lot of things to do here.

In the centre there's a beautiful old castle. There are guided tours every day, and there's a café in the castle garden.

There's a swimming pool near the school, but there isn't a cinema. If you want to see a film, there's a bus to the city every hour.

Is there a hotel? Yes, there is — there's one small hotel with fifteen rooms. There aren't many tourists in winter, so it's very quiet.

Вопросы по прослушанному

Excuse me, is there a supermarket near here? Yes, there is. It's on Green Street, next to the bank. Thank you. And are there any good cafés? There are two cafés on the square. There's a nice one with a garden. Is there a bus to the station? Yes, there's a bus every fifteen minutes.
