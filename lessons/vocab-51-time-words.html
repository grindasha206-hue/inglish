A · Time in relation to now

Время относительно «сейчас»

Нажмите на слово — перевод и 🔊.

Now means at this moment. Then means at another moment (usually in the past).

I was born in Edinburgh. Then we moved to London. Now I live in Cambridge.

two years: 2008–2010 = for two yearsв течение двух лет · from … toс … до : from 2008 to 2010

last yearв прошлом году / last week / last Saturday

next yearв следующем году / next week / next summer

It is July now. last monthв прошлом месяце : Last month it was June. next monthв следующем месяце : Next month it will be August.

When we talk about time in general, we talk about the pastпрошлое , the presentнастоящее and the futureбудущее .

In the past people didn't have television.

People may travel to Mars in the future.

C · Expressions

Выражения

Notice the use of a in these expressions of frequency.

once a weekраз в неделю [one time]: I go swimming once a week, every Saturday.

twice a dayдважды в день [two times]: I clean my teeth twice a day.

three times a yearтри раза в год : I see my uncle three times a year.

four times a month: I play football four or five times a month.

I'll be with you in a momentчерез минутку . [a very short time]
