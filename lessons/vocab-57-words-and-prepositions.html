B · Phrasal verbs

Фразовые глаголы с look

LOOK
  • at → a book / old photographs
  • for → a key
  • after → children
  • forward to → a party / a holiday

Some verbs have different meanings when they are used with different prepositions, for example, look.

I love looking atрассматривать old photographs.

If you want to find your key, you must look forискать it.

Parents look afterзаботиться о their children. [they take care of them]

You look forward toждать с нетерпением something nice in the future, for example, a friend's visit, or a holiday.

C · Adjectives

Прилагательные с предлогами

Some adjectives are also followed by prepositions.

I'm good atхорошо умеющий geography but bad atплохо умеющий maths.

I'm interested inинтересующийся (hearing) all your news.

He is afraid ofбоящийся mice.

Lucy is proud ofгордый winning a medal and her mother is proud of her.

Note: You are used toпривыкший к what you know well: I'm used to getting up early, I always do.

You have to get used toпривыкнуть к something new, for example a new school or driving on the other side of the road.

Error warning
She's a lovely person and I'm really happy about her success [NOT happy for her success].
D · Grammar

Грамматика

Prepositions are followed by a noun: Joe is good at tennis

or by the -ing form of the verb: Joe is good at playing the piano.

Tip
Look carefully at prepositions when you read in English. Make a note of any phrases which use prepositions in a new way.
