A · What are phrasal verbs?

Что такое фразовые глаголы

Нажмите на глагол — перевод и 🔊.

phrasal verbsфразовые глаголы have two parts: a verb + a particle.

get up / on / off

I got up at 6.30 this morning. I'm tired now. (get upвставать )

We should get on the bus. It's leaving in five minutes! (get onсадиться (в автобус) )

We got off the bus at the City Museum. (get offвыходить (из автобуса) )

turn on / off / up / down

He always turns on the TV at 9 o'clock to watch the news. (turn onвключать )

It's a sunny day. Turn the light off. (turn offвыключать )

Turn the TV up. I can't hear it. (turn upсделать громче )

Turn the TV down. It's too loud. (turn downсделать тише; отказаться )

go on / off

Don't stop. Go on talking. It's very interesting. [continue] (go onпродолжать )

Karen went off and forgot her handbag. [left] (go offуйти, уехать )

put something on: It's cold and windy outside. Put your coat on. / Put on your coat. (put onнадевать )

come on: Come on!Давай! Скорее! We're late.

B · One phrasal verb, different meanings

Разные значения

Note that one phrasal verb can often have different meanings.

turn down: She turned down the stereo. [made it not so loud] She turned down the invitation. [refused it]

take off: Our plane takes off (take offвзлетать; снимать ) at 12.30. [leaves the ground] She took off her shoes. [removed them from her feet]

Tip
Make a special page in your notebook. Write down any phrasal verbs you see or hear. Organise them into groups, in any way that makes sense to you, for example, clothes, movement.
