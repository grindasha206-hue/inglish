A · a/an и some

a/an + singular countable nouns (car/apple/shoe etc.):

  • I need a new car.
  • Would you like an apple?
an apple

some + plural countable nouns (cars/apples/shoes etc.):

  • I need some new shoes.
  • Would you like some apples?
some apples

some + uncountable nouns (water/money/music etc.):

  • I need some water.
  • Would you like some cheese?
    (or Would you like a piece of cheese?)
some cheese or
a piece of cheese

Compare a и some:

  • Nicola bought a hat, some shoes and some perfume.
  • I read a newspaper, made some phone calls, and listened to some music.

B · Иногда countable, иногда uncountable

a cake
some cakes
some cake or a piece of cake
a chicken
some chickens
some chicken or a piece of chicken

Compare a paper (= a newspaper) и some paper:

  • I want something to read. I'm going to buy a paper.
  • but I want to make a shopping list. I need some paper / a piece of paper. (не a paper)

C · Осторожно

advice   bread   furniture   hair   information   news   weather   work

Эти существительные обычно uncountable. Нельзя сказать a/an … a bread, an advice, и у них нет множественного числа advices, furnitures etc.

  • Can I talk to you? I need some advice. (не an advice)
  • I'm going to buy some bread. (не a bread)
  • They've got some nice furniture in their house. (не furnitures)
  • Silvia has very long hair. (не hairs)
  • Where can I get some information about hotels here? (не informations)
  • Listen! I've just had some good news. (не a good news)
  • It's nice weather today. (не a nice weather)
  • 'Do you like your job?' 'Yes, but it's hard work.' (не a hard work)

Говорим a job (но не a work):

  • I've got a new job. (не a new work)
