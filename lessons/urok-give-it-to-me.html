A · give / lend / pass / send / show

give   lend   pass   send   show

После этих глаголов (give/lend и т.д.) возможны две структуры:

give something to somebody

  • I gave the keys to Sarah.

give somebody something

  • I gave Sarah the keys.

Также можно сказать buy/get somebody something:

  • I bought my mother some flowers. (= I bought some flowers for my mother.)
  • I'm going to the shop. Can I get you anything? (= get anything for you)

D · порядок слов

Можно сказать:

  • I gave the keys to Sarah.
    and I gave Sarah the keys.
    (но не I gave to Sarah the keys)
  • That's my book. Can you give it to me?
    and Can you give me that book?
    (но не Can you give to me that book?)

С it или them предпочитаем первую структуру (give something to somebody):

  • I gave it to her. (не I gave her it)
  • Here are the keys. Give them to your father. (не Give your father them)

→ it/him/them etc. — Unit 59
