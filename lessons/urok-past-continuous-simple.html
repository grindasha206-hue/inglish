  • Anna read a magazine yesterday. (= from beginning to end)

  • Anna was reading a magazine when I came in.

Вместе в одном рассказе

  • I started work at 8.30 and finished at 5. At 12 o'clock I was working.
  • It was snowing when we left the house. (= it started snowing before we left)
  • I saw Mark and Olga in the park. They were walking their dog.
  • Tom fell asleep while he was watching a film.

Paula and the ladder

Найдите, что было в процессе (was/were + -ing), а что случилось (past simple).

Last Sunday my sister Paula was painting her kitchen. She was standing on a ladder when her phone rang.

She turned round quickly, and the ladder moved. Paula fell and hurt her arm.

At that moment I was walking along the road with my dog. I heard a noise, so I ran to her house.

When I arrived, Paula was sitting on the floor. I called a taxi, and we went to the hospital. Luckily, she didn't break her arm.
