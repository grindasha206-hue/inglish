I like music · I hate exams

Без the: общие понятия, спорт, языки, предметы; the — конкретное

Presentation

I like music · I hate exams

A · Без the — общие понятия

the не используем для общих понятий:

  • I like music, especially classical music.
    (не the music … the classical music)
  • We don't eat meat very often. (не the meat)
  • Life is not possible without water.
    (не The life … the water)
  • I hate exams. (не the exams)
  • Is there a shop near here that sells newspapers?

the не используем для игр и спорта:

  • My favourite sports are football and skiing. (не the football … the skiing)

the не используем для языков и школьных предметов (history/geography/physics/biology etc.):

  • Do you think English is difficult? (не the English)
  • Tom's brother is studying physics and chemistry.
the

B · flowers или the flowers?

Compare:

  • Flowers are beautiful.
    (= flowers in general)
  • I don't like cold weather.
    (= cold weather in general)
  • We don't eat fish very often.
    (= fish in general)
  • Are you interested in history?
    (= history in general)
  • I love this garden.
    The flowers are beautiful.
    (= the flowers in this garden)
  • The weather isn't very good today.
    (= the weather today)
  • We had a great meal last night. The fish was excellent.
    (= the fish we ate last night)
  • Do you know much about the history of your country?
