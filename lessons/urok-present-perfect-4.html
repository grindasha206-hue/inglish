A
Presentation

Как долго?

Сравните: где Хелен сейчас и как долго она там находится.

Helen is on holiday in Ireland. She is there now.
She arrived in Ireland on Monday. Today is Thursday.

How long has she been in Ireland?

She has been in Ireland { since Monday. / for three days. }

My friend Lena

Как долго Лена делает то, что делает? Найдите since и for.

My friend Lena is a teacher. She has been a teacher for twelve years, and she has worked at the same school since 2015.

She has lived in a small village near Oryol all her life. She has known her husband since they were children. They have been married for eight years.

Lena loves music. She has been learning the piano for two years, and she has had her own piano since last April.

Right now she is in the garden. She has been working there since nine o'clock this morning!
