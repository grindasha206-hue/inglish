Prefixesприставки (at the beginning of words) can help you to understand what a new word means. Here are some common prefixes.

prefixmeaningexamples
ex (+ noun)was but now isn'tex-wife, ex-president
half (+ noun or adjective)50% of somethinghalf-price, half-hour
in, im (+ adjective)notinformal, impossible
non (+ adjective or noun)notnon-smoking
pre (+ noun, adjective, verb)beforepre-school, pre-heat
re (+ verb)againredo, rewrite
un (+ adjective or noun)notunhappy, unsafe

An ex-wifeбывшая жена is a wife who is now divorcedразведённый .

An ex-presidentбывший президент is someone who was a president before.

Something that cost £10 yesterday and costs £5 today is half-priceза полцены .

A half-hourполучасовой journey is a journey of 30 minutes.

informalнеофициальный clothes are clothes like jeans and trainers. formalофициальный clothes are things like a suit.

If something is impossibleневозможный , you can't do it. It's impossible to read with your eyes closed.

You must not smoke in a non-smokingдля некурящих restaurant.

pre-schoolдошкольный children are too young to go to school.

You nearly always need to pre-heatразогреть заранее the oven before you cook something.

To redoпеределать something is to do it a second time, and to rewriteпереписать something is to write it a second time.

unhappyнесчастный means sad, the opposite of happy.

unsafeнебезопасный means dangerous, the opposite of safe.

This bridge is unsafe!

Tip
Sometimes words with prefixes have a hyphen (-), e.g. a half-hour programme, and sometimes they don't, e.g. an impossible question. Use a dictionary when you are not sure if there is a hyphen or not.
