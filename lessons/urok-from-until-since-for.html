A · from … to …

  • We lived in Japan from 2003 to 2010.
  • I work from Monday to Friday.

Можно сказать и from … until …:

  • We lived in Japan from 2003 until 2010.

Можно сказать till (= until):

Сравните:

  • 'How long will you be away?' 'Until Monday.'
  • 'When are you coming back?' 'On Monday.'

Four people

Найдите from … to, until, since.

Alex lived in Canada from 2001 to 2009. He came to England in 2009, so he has lived in England since 2009 — for many years now.

Karen lived in France until 2011. Then she came to Switzerland. She has lived in Switzerland since 2011.

Clare worked in a restaurant from 2010 to 2012. She has worked in a hotel since 2012.

Adam was a teacher from 2002 until 2008. He has been a journalist since 2008.
