I am, I don't etc.

Короткие ответы без повторения: but he is, but she doesn't, Yes, I am

A
Presentation

I am, I don't etc.

A · Не повторяем слова

  • She isn't tired, but he is.
    (he is = he is tired)

— Do you like tea?

DAVID
Yes, I do.
No, I don't.
LISA
  • He likes tea, but she doesn't.
    (she doesn't = she doesn't like tea)

В этих примерах не нужно повторять слова (he is tired, she doesn't like tea).

Так же можно использовать эти глаголы:

am/is/are
was/were
have/has
do/does/did
can
will
might
must
  • I haven't got a car, but my sister has. (= my sister has got a car)
  • A: Please help me.
    B: I'm sorry. I can't. (= I can't help you)
  • A: Are you tired?
    B: I was, but I'm not now. (= I was tired, but I'm not tired now)
  • A: Do you think Laura will come and see us?
    B: She might. (= she might come)
  • A: Are you going now?
    B: Yes, I'm afraid I must. (= I must go)

Краткие формы 'm / 's / 've так не используются — нужно am / is / have:

  • She isn't tired, but he is. (не but he's)

А отрицательные краткие формы isn't / haven't / won't можно:

  • My sister has got a car, but I haven't.
  • 'Are you and Jane working tomorrow?' 'I am, but Jane isn't.'

B · После Yes и No

После Yes и No можно сказать I am / I'm not и т.д.:

  • 'Are you tired?' 'Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.'
  • 'Will Alan be here tomorrow?' 'Yes, he will. / No, he won't.'
  • 'Is there a bus to the airport?' 'Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.'

C · do/does и did

do/does — для present simple:

  • I don't like hot weather, but Sue does. (= Sue likes hot weather)
  • Sue works hard, but I don't. (= I don't work hard)
  • 'Do you enjoy your work?' 'Yes, I do.'

did — для past simple:

  • A: Did you and Chris enjoy the film?
    B: I did, but Chris didn't. (= I enjoyed it, but Chris didn't enjoy it)
  • 'I had a good time.' 'I did too.' (= I enjoyed it too)
  • 'Did it rain yesterday?' 'No, it didn't.'

My brother and I

Найдите все места, где глагол стоит без повтора (but he isn't, but I did).

My brother Max and I are very different. I'm tall, but he isn't. He likes football, but I don't. I can cook, but he can't.

Max has got a car, but I haven't. I go to work by bus. I was a good student at school, but Max wasn't. He didn't like school, but I did.

Last summer we went to Italy together. I enjoyed the trip, but Max didn't — it was too hot for him.

Is Max a good brother? Yes, he is! He's always ready to help. Will we go on holiday together again? I hope we will.

Sounds & Speech

Ударение в кратких ответах

В кратких ответах глагол звучит с ударением: but he is.

1 · Ударение на глаголе

She isn't tired, but he is.
He likes tea, but she doesn't.

2 · Yes / No + краткий ответ

Are you tired? — Yes, I am.
Will Alan be here? — No, he won't.

3 · do / did

I don't like hot weather, but Sue does.
I did, but Chris didn't.
