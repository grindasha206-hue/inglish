will / shall 2

I'll … — предложения и решения; Shall I …? Shall we …?

A
Presentation

I'll … — предложение и решение в момент речи

A · I'll … когда предлагаем или решаем что-то сделать

I'll … (I will) используем, когда предлагаем что-то или решаем что-то сделать:

  • 'This box is very heavy.' — 'I'll help you.'
  • 'I'll call you tonight, OK?' — 'OK, bye.'

Часто говорим I think I'll … / I don't think I'll …, когда решаем что-то сделать:

  • I'm hungry. I think I'll make a sandwich.
  • It's sunny. I think I'll walk to work.
  • It's snowing. I don't think I'll go out.

Не используем present simple (I go / I phone etc.) в таких предложениях:

  • I'll call you tonight, OK? (не I call you)
  • I think I'll go to bed early. (не I go to bed)

B · I'll не используем для того, что решили раньше (→ Units 25–26)

  • I'm working on Saturday. (не I'll work)
  • I don't need my bike any more. I'm going to sell it. (не I'll sell)
  • What are you doing at the weekend? (не What will you do)

C · Shall I …? Shall we …?

Shall I / Shall we …? = Как ты думаешь, это хорошая мысль? Сделать мне / нам это?

  • It's very hot in here. Shall I open the window?
  • 'Shall I call you tomorrow?' — 'Yes, please.'
  • I'm going to a wedding. What shall I wear?
  • It's a lovely day. Shall we go to the park?
  • Where shall we go on holiday this year?
  • 'Let's have dinner together.' — 'OK, what time shall we meet?'

Moving day

Найдите I'll и Shall I / Shall we. Кто что предлагает?

It was Saturday morning, and Kate was moving to a new flat. There were boxes everywhere.

'This box is really heavy,' she said. 'Don't worry, I'll carry it,' said her brother Max. 'Shall I put it in the kitchen?' 'Yes, please.'

At one o'clock everybody was hungry. 'I'll make some sandwiches,' said Kate's friend Olga. 'Shall we order pizza instead?' asked Max. 'Good idea! I'll call the pizza place.'

In the evening Kate was tired. 'I don't think I'll unpack any more today,' she said. 'I think I'll have a bath and go to bed.'
