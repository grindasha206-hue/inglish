every and all

every + ед. ч.; every day / all day; everybody, everything, everywhere

A
Presentation

every and all

A · every

Every house in the street is the same.

every house in the street =
all the houses in the street

every + singular noun (every house / every country etc.):

  • Sarah has been to every country in Europe.
  • Every summer we have a holiday by the sea.
  • She looks different every time I see her.

После every … — глагол в единственном числе:

  • Every house in the street is the same. (не are the same)
  • Every country has a national flag. (не have)

Compare every и all:

  • Every student in the class passed the exam.
  • Every country has a national flag.
  • All the students in the class passed the exam.
  • All countries have a national flag.

C · everybody (or everyone) / everything / everywhere

everybody or everyone
(people)		Everybody (or Everyone) needs friends.
(= all people need friends)
everything
(things)		Do you have everything you need?
(= all the things you need)
everywhere
(places)		I lost my watch. I've looked everywhere for it.
(= I've looked in all places)

После everybody/everyone/everything — глагол в единственном числе:

  • Everybody has problems. (не Everybody have)

Summer by the sea

Найдите every, all, everybody, everything, everywhere.

Every summer my family goes to the seaside. Everybody loves it — my parents, my sister and me.

We stay at the same hotel every year. Every room has a minibar and a sea view. Everything is perfect!

Last summer it was very hot. We swam every morning and lay on the beach all afternoon. On the last day it rained all day, so we stayed inside.

Everyone at the hotel is friendly. The staff know every guest by name. I've been everywhere in that town!
