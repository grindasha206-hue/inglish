A
Presentation

not as … as

A · not as … as

ANNA
I'm 93.
I'm 96.
KATE

She's old, but she's not as old as he is.

Box A isn't as big as Box B.
  • Rome is not as old as Athens.
    (= Athens is older)
  • The Grand Hotel isn't as expensive as the Europa.
    (= the Europa is more expensive)
  • I don't play tennis as often as you.
    (= you play more often)
  • The weather is better than it was yesterday. It isn't as cold.
    (= as cold as it was yesterday)

B · not as much as … / not as many as …

  • I don't have as much money as you. (= you've got more money)
  • I don't know as many people as you. (= you know more people)
  • I don't go out as much as you. (= you go out more)

C · Compare not as … as и than

  • Rome is not as old as Athens.
    Athens is older than Rome. (не older as Rome)
  • Tennis isn't as popular as football.
    Football is more popular than tennis.
  • I don't go out as much as you.
    You go out more than me.

D · as me / as him / as her etc.

Обычно говорим: as me / as him / as her etc. Можно также:

  • She's not as old as him. or She's not as old as he is.
  • You don't work as hard as me. or You don't work as hard as I do.

E · the same as …

  • The weather today is the same as yesterday.
  • My hair is the same colour as yours.
  • I arrived at the same time as Tom.

Reading

Olga and I

Найдите not as … as, than и the same as.

My friend Olga and I are very different. I'm not as tall as her, and I don't run as fast as she does.

Olga reads a lot. I don't read as many books as her, but I watch more films. She doesn't have as much free time as me — she has two jobs!

My flat isn't as big as Olga's, but it's in the centre. Her flat is further from work.

But some things are the same. We were born in the same year, and my hair is the same colour as hers!

Is Oryol as big as Moscow? No, it's not as big as Moscow. Moscow is much bigger. Is it as expensive? No, not as expensive. Life is cheaper in Oryol. And the weather? It's about the same as in Moscow.

Мы разные

Сравните себя с другом в 6–8 предложениях: I'm not as … as …, I don't … as much / many … as …, и что у вас the same.

  • I'm not as … as …
  • I don't … as often / much as …
  • … is … than me.
  • Our … is the same.
