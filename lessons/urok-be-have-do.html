Вспомогательные глаголы во всех пройденных временах

A
Presentation

be, have, do — повторяем всё вместе

Три вспомогательных глагола и все времена, которые мы прошли.

A · be (= am/is/are/was/were) + -ing (cleaning / working etc.)

am/is/are + -ing
(present continuous)
→ Units 3–4 и 25
  • Please be quiet. I'm studying.
  • It isn't snowing at the moment.
  • What are you doing tonight?
was/were + -ing
(past continuous)
→ Unit 13
  • I was cooking when he called.
  • It wasn't raining, so we didn't take an umbrella.
  • What were you doing at 8 o'clock?

B · be + past participle (cleaned / made / eaten etc.)

am/is/are + past participle
(passive present simple)
→ Unit 21
  • I'm never asked about it.
  • Cheese is made from milk.
  • These rooms aren't cleaned every day.
was/were + past participle
(passive past simple)
→ Unit 21
  • The kitchen was cleaned yesterday.
  • These bridges were built 50 years ago.
  • How was the cup broken?
  • Where were you born?

C · have/has + past participle (cleaned / lost / eaten / been etc.)

have/has + past participle
(present perfect)
→ Units 15–18
  • I've washed the car.
  • Anna has lost her phone.
  • Max hasn't been to Spain.
  • Where have Kate and Tom gone?

D · do/does/did + infinitive (clean / like / eat / go etc.)

do/does + infinitive
(present simple negative and questions)
→ Units 6–7
  • I like tea, but I don't like coffee.
  • Olga doesn't eat meat.
  • What do you usually do on Sundays?
  • Does Ben live with his parents?
did + infinitive
(past simple negative and questions)
→ Unit 12
  • I didn't go out last night.
  • It didn't snow last winter.
  • What time did Max and Anna arrive?

A letter from Kazan

Найдите все формы be, have и do. Какое время в каждом предложении?

Hi Masha! I'm writing to you from Kazan. I'm staying here for a week with my friend Lena.

We arrived on Monday. The weather was terrible — it was raining when we got off the train, and we didn't have umbrellas!

Kazan is a beautiful city. The Kremlin was built in the 16th century, and it is visited by thousands of tourists every day. I've already taken more than 200 photos.

Lena doesn't like museums, so she isn't coming with me today. She's shopping. Have you ever been to Kazan? Do you want to come next year? Love, Anya

be, have, do на слух

Слушайте и повторяйте.

1 · be — am / is / are / was / were

I'm working.
It was raining.

2 · have / has

I've cleaned my room.

3 · do / does / did

Chris doesn't go out very often.
What time did they go out?

Письмо другу

Напишите письмо другу (6–8 предложений): что вы делаете сейчас, что делали вчера, что уже сделали, что обычно делаете. Используйте разные времена.

  • I'm … at the moment
  • Yesterday I … / I was … when …
  • I've already … / I haven't … yet
  • I usually … / I don't …
