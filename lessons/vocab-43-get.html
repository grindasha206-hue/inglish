It's light. → It's getting dark. → It's dark. (get darkтемнеть )

She's ill. → She's getting better. → She's better. / She's well. (get betterвыздоравливать )

I'm getting tired. I want to go to bed. (get tiredуставать )

It's raining! I'm getting wet! (get wetпромокнуть )

B · Get with nouns

get + существительное

If you don't have something you can get it. [get = buy or find] (getстановиться; получать , gotстал, получил (прош. от get) )

I want to send a postcard. I have to get a stampкупить марку .

I'm going to the shop to get a newspaperкупить газету .

Do you want a drink? I can get some coffee.

Where can I get a taxiпоймать такси ?

I've finished my studies. Now I want to get a jobнайти работу .

My friend is ill! Please get a doctorвызвать врача .

C · Expressions

Выражения с get

Maria and David are getting married in April. (get marriedпожениться )

A: When you get toдобраться до New York, call me. [arrive at, reach] B: OK, give me your number.

A: How can I get to the airport? B: Take the airport bus at the bus station.

I'll see you when you get back from Hong Kong. [return, come home] (get backвернуться )

Error warning
When I get home, I have my lunch [NOT When I get to home].
I get there at 6 o'clock, so please ring me at 6.30 [NOT I get to there]. (get homeдобраться домой , get thereдобраться туда )
