What are you doing tomorrow?

Present continuous для планов на будущее

A
Presentation

Сейчас — и в будущем по плану

Сравните ситуации и обратите внимание на форму глагола.

DAVID
I'm playing tennis tomorrow.

A · am / is / are + -ing — сейчас и в будущем

am/is/are + -ing (present continuous) — то, что происходит сейчас:

  • 'Where are Max and Olga?' — 'They're swimming in the pool.'
  • Please be quiet. I'm reading.

am/is/are + -ing также используем для будущего (tomorrow / next week etc.):

  • Anna is playing tennis tomorrow.
  • I'm not working next week.

B · I am doing something tomorrow = я договорился, у меня есть план

LISA
I'm going to a concert tomorrow.
  • Kate is going to the doctor on Friday. (= she has an appointment with the doctor)
  • We're having a party next Saturday.
  • Are you meeting your friends tonight?
  • What are you doing tomorrow evening?
  • I'm not going out tonight. I'm staying at home.

Можно также сказать «I'm going to do something» (→ Unit 26).

C · present continuous — договорённости; present simple — расписания

Present continuous (I'm staying / are you coming etc.) — о том, о чём человек договорился:

  • I'm staying at home this evening. (не I stay)
  • Are you going out tonight? (не Do you go)
  • Max isn't coming to the party next week. (не Max doesn't come)

Но present simple (start, arrives etc.) — для расписаний: timetables, programmes, trains, buses etc.:

  • The bus leaves at 8.15.
  • What time does the concert start?

Compare

present continuous (обычно о людях)

I'm going to the theatre tomorrow.
What time are you leaving?

present simple (расписания, программы)

The play starts at 7.00.
What time does your bus leave?

D
Reading

Olga's busy week

Где договорённость (present continuous), а где расписание (present simple)?

Next week is going to be very busy for Olga. On Monday she's starting a new English course. The lessons start at 6.30 every Monday and Wednesday.

On Tuesday she's meeting her old school friend for lunch, and in the evening she's going to the dentist — she has an appointment at 7.

On Friday she's flying to Saint Petersburg for the weekend. Her plane leaves at 9.15 in the morning and arrives at 10.45.

She's staying with her cousin, and on Saturday evening they're going to a concert. 'I'm not doing anything on Sunday,' she says. 'I'm sleeping!'
