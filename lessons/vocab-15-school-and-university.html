C · Expressions

Выражения

A maths teacher teachesпреподаёт maths. Her studentsстуденты studyизучать maths.

Children go to schoolходить в школу and students go to universityучиться в университете .

At school children learnучиться, учить to read and write.

Students can do an (English) course in many schools and universities. At the end of a course, you often take / do an exam. You hope to pass your exams. You don't want to fail your exams.

If you pass your final exams at university, you get a degreeполучить диплом .

Фразы: do a courseпроходить курс , take an examсдавать экзамен , pass an examсдать экзамен , fail an examпровалить экзамен .
