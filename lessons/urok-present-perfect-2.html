I've just… I've already… I haven't… yet (present perfect 2)

Только что, уже и ещё не

A
Presentation

just, already, yet

Три слова, которые часто встречаются с present perfect.

A · I've just …

just = a short time ago (только что)

  • A: Are Kate and Max here?
    B: Yes, they've just arrived.
  • A: Are you hungry?
    B: No, I've just had lunch.
  • A: Is Sam here?
    B: No, I'm afraid he's just gone. (= he has just gone)

B · I've already …

already = before you expected / before I expected (уже — раньше, чем ожидали)

  • A: What time are Kate and Max coming?
    B: They've already arrived. (= before you expected)
  • It's only 8 o'clock and Tom has already gone to bed. (= before I expected)
  • A: Olga, this is Peter.
    B: Yes, I know. We've already met.

C · I haven't … yet / Have you … yet?

yet = until now (ещё / уже — до этого момента)
Yet используется в отрицаниях и вопросах. Обычно yet стоит в конце.

yet в отрицаниях (I haven't … yet)

  • A: Are Kate and Max here?
    B: No, they haven't arrived yet. (but B expects them to arrive soon)
  • A: Does your boss know that you're leaving?
    B: No, I haven't told her yet. (but B is going to tell her soon)
  • Lena has bought a new coat, but she hasn't worn it yet.

yet в вопросах (Have you … yet?)

  • A: Have Kate and Max arrived yet?
    B: No, not yet. We're still waiting for them.
  • A: Has Ben started his new job yet?
    B: No, he starts next Monday.
  • A: This is my new bag.
    B: Oh, it's nice. Have you used it yet?

Reading

Mia's new flat

Найдите just, already и yet. Что Миа уже сделала, а что — ещё нет?

Mia has just moved to a new flat. She arrived on Saturday morning, and it's Sunday evening now.

She has already unpacked most of the boxes, and she has already cleaned the kitchen. Her friend Lucy has just brought her some flowers.

But Mia hasn't finished yet. She hasn't put up the curtains yet, and the fridge isn't working. She has called a plumber, but he hasn't come yet.

'Have you met your neighbours yet?' asks Lucy. 'Yes, I've already met them,' says Mia. 'They're very nice. They've just invited me for dinner!'

Sounds & Speech

Где ударение

just и already звучат перед смысловым глаголом, yet — в самом конце, с понижением голоса.

1 · just

They've just arrived.
I've just had lunch.

2 · already /ɔːlˈredi/

We've already met.
She's already gone to bed.

3 · yet /jet/

I haven't finished yet.
Have you seen it yet? — No, not yet.
