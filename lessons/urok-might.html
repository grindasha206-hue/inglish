might

Возможно: might, might not, may

A
Presentation

might — возможно

A · might

He might go to New York.
(= it is possible that he will go to New York)

It might rain.
(= it is possible that it will rain)

might + infinitive (might go / might be / might rain etc.):

I/we/you/they
he/she/it		might (not)be
go
play
come etc.

B · I might = возможно, я …

  • I might go to the theatre tonight, but I'm not sure. (= it is possible that I will go)
  • A: When is Olga going to call you?
    B: I don't know. She might call this evening.
  • Take a coat with you. It might be cold.
  • Buy a lottery ticket. You might be lucky. (= perhaps you will be lucky)
  • A: Are you going to the party?
    B: I might. (= I might go)

Study the difference

  • I'm meeting Anna tomorrow. (sure)
    I might meet Anna tomorrow. (possible)
  • Max is going to call later. (sure)
    Max might call later. (possible)

C · I might not = возможно, я не …

  • I might not go to work tomorrow. (= it is possible that I will not go)
  • Kate might not come to the meeting. (= it is possible that she will not come)

D · may

may можно использовать так же. I may = I might:

  • I may go to the theatre tonight. (= I might go)
  • Kate may not come to the meeting. (= Kate might not come)

May I …? = Is it OK to …? / Can I …?:

  • May I ask a question? (= is it OK to ask / can I ask?)
  • 'May I sit here?' — 'Yes, of course.'

Reading

Alina's summer

Что Алина, возможно, будет делать летом? Найдите might.

Alina doesn't know what to do this summer. She has a few ideas, but she isn't sure yet.

She might go to the sea with her friends. Or she might visit her grandparents in the village — they haven't seen her for a long time.

She might also take an English course in Malta, but it's expensive, so she might not have enough money.

'Maybe I'll just stay at home and relax,' she laughs. 'Or I might buy a lottery ticket. I might be lucky!'
