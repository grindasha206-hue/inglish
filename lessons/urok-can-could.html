A · can + infinitive

He can play the piano.

B · I can do something = я умею или у меня есть возможность

  • I can play the guitar. My sister can play the guitar too.
  • Olga can speak French, but she can't speak German.
  • A: Can you ski?
    B: Yes, but I'm not very good.
  • A: Can you change a thousand roubles?
    B: I'm sorry, I can't.
  • I'm having a party on Friday, but Max and Kate can't come.

C · Для прошлого (yesterday / last week etc.) — could / couldn't

  • When I was a child, I could swim very well.
  • Before Tom moved to Spain, he couldn't speak Spanish. Now he can speak it very well.
  • I was very tired last night, but I couldn't sleep.
  • I had a party last week, but Max and Kate couldn't come.

D · Can you …? Could you …? Can I …? Could I …?

Can you …? или Could you …? — когда просим людей что-то сделать:

  • Can you close the window, please? или Could you close the window, please?
  • Can you help me, please? или Could you help me…?

Can I have …? или Can I get …? — когда просим что-то:

  • Can I have a cup of coffee, please? или Can I get …?

Can I …? или Could I …? = можно мне …?:

  • Can I use your phone?
  • Anna, could I borrow your pen?

Reading

My amazing grandmother

Что бабушка умеет сейчас (can) и умела раньше (could)?

My grandmother is eighty, but she can do a lot of things. She can use a smartphone, she can bake amazing cakes, and she can speak three languages.

When she was young, she could run very fast, and she could swim across the river. Now she can't run, but she can still walk ten kilometres a day!

She couldn't read until she was eight, because there was no school in her village. But now she reads a book every week.

Yesterday I asked her: 'Grandma, could you teach me to bake your cake?' 'Of course I can,' she said. 'Can you come on Sunday?'

Sounds & Speech

can и could на слух

Слушайте и повторяйте.

1 · can /kən/ — слабая форма; can't /kɑːnt/ — полная

В утверждении can звучит коротко, can't — с долгим /ɑː/.

I can swim.
I can't swim.

2 · could /kʊd/ и couldn't /ˈkʊdnt/

When I was young, I could run very fast.
I couldn't sleep last night.

3 · Просьбы ↗

Could you open the door, please?
Can I have a glass of water, please?

Вопросы по прослушанному

Excuse me, could you help me? Of course. What's the problem? I can't find the station. Can you show me on the map? Sure. It's here, about two kilometres away. Can I walk there? Yes, you can. But you could also take bus 5. Thank you! Could I borrow your pen to write the number?
