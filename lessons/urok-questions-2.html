B · who/what — subject или object

В этих вопросах who/what — подлежащее (subject):

  • Who lives in this house? (= somebody lives in it — who?)
    (не Who does live?)
  • What happened? (= something happened — what?)
    (не What did happen?)
  • What's happening? (What's = What is)
  • Who's got my key? (Who's = Who has)

В этих вопросах who/what — дополнение (object):

  • Who did you meet yesterday? (= you met somebody — who?)
  • What did Paul say? (= Paul said something — what?)
  • Who are you phoning?
  • What was Silvia wearing?

Compare:

George likes oranges.→Who likes oranges? — George.
What does George like? — Oranges.
Jane won a new car.→Who won a new car? — Jane.
What did Jane win? — A new car.

C · who и what

who — для людей (somebody). what — для вещей, идей и т.д. (something):

  • Who is your favourite singer?
  • What is your favourite song?

Who broke the window?

Найдите вопросы. Где who/what — подлежащее, где — дополнение?

Something happened at our office yesterday. Who broke the window? Nobody knows!

At nine o'clock Lisa came in and saw the broken window. 'What happened?' she asked. 'Who did this?'

Then she phoned the boss. 'Who are you phoning?' asked Mark. 'What did the boss say?' 'He said: who was here last night?'

Who found the answer? The cleaner! She saw a big bird. It flew into the window. So who broke the window? A bird!
