Нужно, нельзя, не нужно

A
Presentation

must, mustn't, don't need to

A · must + infinitive

must + infinitive (must do / must work etc.):

I/we/you/they
he/she/it		mustdo
go
see
eat etc.

B · I must (do something) = мне нужно это сделать

  • I'm very thirsty. I must drink something.
  • It's a wonderful book. You must read it.
  • The car is very dirty. We must wash it.

Для прошлого (yesterday / last week etc.) используем had to … (не must):

  • I was very thirsty. I had to drink something. (не I must drink)
  • We had to take a taxi last night. There were no buses. (не We must take)

C · mustn't (= must not)

I mustn't (do something) = нельзя это делать, это неправильно:

  • I must go. I mustn't be late.
  • I mustn't forget to call Anna. (= I must remember to call her)
  • Be happy! You mustn't be sad. (= don't be sad)
  • You mustn't touch the paintings. (= don't touch the paintings)

D · don't need to

I don't need (to do something) = в этом нет необходимости:

  • I don't need to go yet. I can stay a little longer.
  • You don't need to shout. I can hear you OK.

Можно также сказать don't have to …:

  • I don't have to go yet. I can stay a little longer.

Compare don't need to и mustn't

  • You don't need to go. You can stay here if you want.
  • You mustn't go. You must stay here.

Hotel rules

Что гостям нужно делать, что нельзя, а что не обязательно?

Welcome to Green Hill Hotel! Here are some important rules for our guests.

You must leave your room by 11 o'clock on the last day. You mustn't smoke in the rooms, and you mustn't bring pets.

Breakfast is free, so you don't need to pay for it. You don't need to reserve a table — just come between 7 and 10.

Last summer one guest lost his key, and he had to pay 20 euros for a new one. So please be careful — you mustn't lose your key!

Mum, I'm going to the cinema with Lena. OK, but you must be home by ten. Do I need to take an umbrella? No, you don't need to. It's sunny. Last time I had to wait for the bus for an hour! Then you mustn't miss the last bus. Call me if there's a problem.
