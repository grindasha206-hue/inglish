quickly/badly/suddenly etc. (adverbs)

A · adjective + -ly → adverb

He ate his dinner very quickly.
Suddenly the shelf fell down.

Quickly и suddenly — adverbs (наречия).

adjective + -ly → adverb:

adjectivequickbadsuddencarefulheavy
adverbquicklybadlysuddenlycarefullyheavilyetc.

Spelling: easy → easily   heavy → heavily

B · Наречия говорят, как что-то происходит

  • The train stopped suddenly.
  • I opened the door slowly.
  • Please listen carefully.
  • I understand you perfectly.
It's raining heavily.

Compare:

adjective

  • Sue is very quiet.
  • Be careful!
  • It was a bad game.
  • I felt nervous.
    (= I was nervous)

adverb

  • Sue speaks very quietly. (не speaks very quiet)
  • Listen carefully! (не listen careful)
  • Our team played badly. (не played bad)
  • I waited nervously.

C · hard / fast / late / early

Эти слова — и прилагательные, и наречия:

  • Sue's job is very hard.
  • Ben is a fast runner.
  • The bus was late/early.
  • Sue works very hard. (не hardly)
  • Ben can run fast.
  • I went to bed late/early.

D · good (adjective) → well (adverb)

  • Your English is very good.
  • It was a good game.
  • You speak English very well. (не very good)
  • Our team played well.

Но well — ещё и прилагательное (= not ill, in good health):

  • 'How are you?' 'I'm very well, thank you. And you?'

My brother the driver

Найдите наречия. Как Макс водит, работает, говорит?

My brother Max is a good driver. He drives carefully and slowly. I'm not a good driver — I drive badly and too fast!

Yesterday it was raining heavily. Suddenly a dog ran into the road. Max stopped quickly and the dog was OK.

Max works very hard. He gets up early and goes to bed late. He speaks English very well, but he speaks quietly.

'How are you?' I asked him last night. 'I'm well, thanks,' he said, 'but I'm very tired.'
