do and make

do — действие, make — создать; выражения с do и make

A
Presentation

do and make

A · do — общее слово для действий

  • What are you doing this evening? (не What are you making?)
  • 'Shall I open the window?' 'No, it's OK. I'll do it.'
  • Rachel's job is very boring. She does the same thing every day.
  • I did a lot of things yesterday.

What do you do? = What's your job?:

  • 'What do you do?' 'I work in a bank.'

B · make = produce / create

She's making coffee.

He has made a cake.

They make umbrellas.

It was made in China.

Compare do and make:

  • I did a lot yesterday. I cleaned my room, I wrote some letters and I made a cake.
  • A: What do you do in your free time? Sport? Reading? Hobbies?
    B: I make clothes. I make dresses and jackets. I also make toys for my children.

C · Выражения с do

doan exam / a test
a course
homework

an exercise
  • I'm doing my driving test next week.
  • John has just done a training course.
  • Our children have to do a lot of homework.
  • I hate doing housework, especially cleaning.
  • Sue, could you do me a favour?
  • I go for a run and do exercises every morning.

also do the shopping / do the washing / do the washing-up / do the ironing / do the cooking etc.:

  • I did the washing but I didn't do the shopping.

D · Выражения с make

makea mistake
an appointment
a phone call
a list

a bed
  • I'm sorry, I made a mistake.
  • I need to make an appointment to see the doctor.
  • Excuse me, I have to make a phone call.
  • Have you made a shopping list?
  • It's late. Don't make a noise.
  • Sometimes I forget to make my bed in the morning.

Говорим make a film, но take a photo / take a picture:

  • When was this film made?
  • but When was this photo taken?

My busy Saturday

Найдите все do и make. Почему здесь do, а здесь make?

Saturday is my busy day. In the morning I do the shopping and make a list for the week. Then I do the housework.

I make my bed, do the washing and do the ironing. My son does his homework, and my daughter makes a cake for us.

In the afternoon I do exercises in the park. My husband makes phone calls to his parents and does the cooking.

In the evening we don't do anything. We watch a film. Do you know when it was made? Nineteen fifty! I made a mistake — I thought it was new.
