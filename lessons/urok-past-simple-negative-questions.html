do / does (present) → did (past)

I don't watch TV very often.→I didn't watch TV yesterday.
Does she often go away?→Did she go away last week?

Sophie's Monday

Найдите в тексте все didn't и вопрос с did.

Last Monday Sophie had an important meeting in the city centre. She left home early, but she didn't take her umbrella.

It started to rain, and she didn't have a coat. She ran to the bus stop, but the bus didn't come.

Finally she took a taxi. When she arrived, the meeting was over. 'Why didn't you call us?' asked her boss.

Sophie looked in her bag. Her phone wasn't there — she didn't bring it! It was on the kitchen table at home.

Sounds & Speech

Did you…? и didn't

Слушайте и повторяйте. Обычная и медленная скорость помогают разобрать фразу.

1 · Did you /ˈdɪdʒə/

В быстрой речи d + y сливаются в звук «дж».

Did you see it?
Did you have a good time?
What did you do?

2 · didn't /ˈdɪdnt/ — под ударением

I didn't know.
She didn't come.

3 · Вопрос и краткий ответ

Да/нет-вопрос — голос вверх ↗, wh-вопрос и ответ — вниз ↘.

Did it rain? — No, it didn't.
Where did you go? — To the mountains.
F
Listening

Слушаем вслепую

Друзья говорят о выходных. Послушайте 1–2 раза и ответьте на вопросы. Скрипт открывайте только для проверки.

Вопросы по прослушанному

Hi, Leo! Did you have a nice weekend? Yes, thanks. I went to the mountains with my brother. Did you stay in a hotel? No, we didn't. We stayed with some friends. Did it rain? No, it didn't. The weather was great. What did you do in the evenings? We cooked dinner and played cards.
