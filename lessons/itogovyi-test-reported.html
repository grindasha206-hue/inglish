At the party

Last Saturday I went to my cousin's birthday party. A lot of people were there, and everybody told me their news.

My cousin Lena said that she was moving to Spain. Her husband told me that he had a new job in Madrid. They said they were very happy.

My aunt said that she couldn't come to the party next year because she would be in Canada. My uncle told me that he didn't like flying.

Lena's friend said that I looked great. I told her that I felt great too — it was a lovely party!

Sounds & Speech

Вопросы на слух

Слушайте и повторяйте. Обычная и медленная скорость помогают разобрать фразу.

1 · was / were / had

She said that she was tired.
He said that he had to leave early.

2 · could / would

Kate said that she couldn't find a job.
Steve said that he would phone me.

3 · didn't / looked

Rachel said that she didn't like her job.
Mike said that I looked tired.

4 · say / tell

He told me that he was tired.
What did she say to you?
