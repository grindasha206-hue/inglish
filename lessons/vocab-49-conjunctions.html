A · Basic conjunctions

Основные союзы

Нажмите на слово — перевод и 🔊.

conjunctionsсоюзы join two parts of a sentence. They help to show the connection between the two parts of the sentence.

wordexampleuse
andи Kate is a student and she works part-time.We use and to give extra information in the second part of the sentence.
butно They are rich but they aren't happy.We use but when the second part of the sentence contrasts with the first part.
orили You can pay by credit card or cash.We use or when the second part of the sentence gives a different possibility.
becauseпотому что We went home early because we were tired.We use because when the second part explains why the first part happened.
soпоэтому I felt ill so I didn't go to work.We use so when the second part gives a result of the first part.
whenкогда I went to the party when the babysitter arrived.We use when to say when the first part of the sentence happened.
beforeперед тем как We left before it started to rain.We use before to show that the first part happened first.
afterпосле того как We went for a meal after we had seen the film.We use after to show that the second part happened first.
ifесли You can have some ice cream if you eat your dinner.We use if to say that the first part will only happen after the second part happens and it may not happen.

Слова: babysitterняня , part-timeнеполный рабочий день .

B · Other connecting words

Другие связующие слова

These words are useful for making connections between words and phrases.

wordexampleuse
onlyтолько He only sleeps for three hours every night.We use only to say something is not very big or very much.
likeкак, похожий на She looks like her father.We use like to make a comparison.
thanчем She works harder than he does.We use than after a comparative adjective or adverb.
alsoтакже He works in the shop and she does also.We use also, too and as well to say something is extra.
tooтоже … and she does too.
as wellтоже, также … and she does as well.
