Presentation

not + any · no · none

A · not + any и no

The car park is empty.

There aren't any cars
There are no cars  } in the car park.

How many cars are there in the car park?
None.

not (-n't) + any

  • There aren't any cars in the car park.
  • Tracey and Jack don't have any children.
  • You can have some coffee, but I don't want any.

no + noun (no cars / no garden etc.)
no … = not any or not a

  • There are no cars in the car park. (= there aren't any cars)
  • We have no coffee. (= we don't have any coffee)
  • It's a nice house, but there's no garden. (= there isn't a garden)

no … — особенно после have и there is/are.

negative verb + any = positive verb + no

  • They don't have any children. or They have no children.
    (не They don't have no children)
  • There isn't any sugar in your coffee. or There's no sugar in your coffee.

B · no и none

no + noun (no money / no children etc.):

  • We have no money.
  • Everything was OK. There were no problems.

none — отдельно (без существительного):

  • 'How much money do you have?' 'None.' (= no money)
  • 'Were there any problems?' 'No, none.' (= no problems)

C · none и no-one

none   = 0 (zero)
no-one = nobody

None — ответ на How much? / How many? (вещи или люди):

  • A: How much money do you have?
    B: None. (= no money)
  • A: How many people did you meet?
    B: None. (= no people)

No-one — ответ на Who?:

  • A: Who did you meet?
    B: No-one. (or Nobody.)

My first flat

Найдите not any, no, none и no-one.

My first flat was very small. There was no garden and there were no shelves. There wasn't any furniture — only a bed!

The kitchen had no fridge, so I didn't have any cold drinks in summer. There was no heating either.

'How many friends visited you?' my mother asked. 'None,' I said. 'It's too small!' 'Who helped you with the move?' 'No-one.'

But I was happy there. I had no money and no problems. Now I have a big flat — and a lot of problems!
