Presentation

A · is / are being … (present continuous passive)

Somebody is painting the door .
(active)
The door is being painted.
(passive)
  • My phone is at the repair shop. It is being repaired. (= somebody is repairing it)
  • A new school is being built near our house. (= somebody is building it)

Compare present continuous и present simple

  • The kitchen is being cleaned at the moment. (continuous)
    The kitchen is cleaned every day. (simple)
  • Concerts are often held in this hall, but no concerts are being held this month.

B · has / have been … (present perfect passive)

before
now
Somebody has painted the door .
(active)
The door has been painted.
(passive)
  • My bag has been stolen. (= somebody has stolen it)
  • My bags have been stolen. (= somebody has stolen them)
  • I'm not going to the wedding. I haven't been invited. (= nobody has invited me)
  • Has this dress been washed? (= has somebody washed it?)

Compare present perfect и past simple

  • The car isn't dirty any more. It has been washed. (present perfect)
    The car was washed yesterday. (past simple)
  • I can't find my phone. I think it's been stolen. (present perfect)
    My phone was stolen last week. (past simple)

Our school

Что сейчас делается (is being done), а что уже сделано (has been done)?

Our school is being renovated this autumn, so the lessons are held in the sports hall.

A lot of work has already been done. The roof has been repaired and the walls in the classrooms have been painted. New computers have been bought for the library.

But the work hasn't been finished yet. The windows are being changed at the moment, and the new floor hasn't been put in.

The headteacher says that everything will be ready in December. The students have been told to bring warm clothes until then!
