A · Saying positive things

Хорошее о людях

Нажмите на слово — перевод и 🔊.

niceмилый, приятный is the most common word used for people who we like / who are good.

Olga's very nice. Richard's a nice man.

If we want to make nice stronger, we can use wonderfulзамечательный .

Ron is a wonderful teacher. All the students love him.

Error warning
Mary is wonderful [NOT Mary is very wonderful].

If someone is good to other people, we use kindдобрый .

She's very kind; she helps me with the children.

My teacher is a lovelyчудесный, милый man. (I like him very much)

My friend Neil is very easy-goingпокладистый . [relaxed, easy to be with]

Maureen's a happyсчастливый person. (opposite: an unhappyнесчастный person)

All my friends are more intelligentумный than me. [cleverумный ]

B · Saying negative things

Плохое о людях

Marcia is not very niceне очень приятный .

Horrible is a lot stronger than 'not very nice'.

Margaret is a horribleужасный, противный woman; nobody likes her.

My uncle is a difficultтрудный (в общении) person. He is never happy.

That waiter is stupidглупый . I asked for coffee and he has given me tea! (stupid is a very strong word)

I don't like selfishэгоистичный people. [people who think only of themselves]

C · Children

Дети

We often say that children are good or well-behavedпослушный . If they are not, we say they are naughtyнепослушный .

Tim is very good / well-behaved, but his sister is very naughty.
