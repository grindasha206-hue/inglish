there is во всех временах

A
Presentation

there was / there has been / there will be

A · there was / there were (past)

There is a train every hour.
The time now is 11.15. There was a train at 11 o'clock.

Compare

there is / are (present)

There is nothing on TV tonight.
We are staying at a big hotel. There are 500 rooms.
Is everything OK? Are there any problems?
I'm hungry, but there isn't anything to eat.

there was / were (past)

There was nothing on TV last night.
We stayed at a big hotel. There were 500 rooms.
Was everything OK yesterday? Were there any problems?
I was hungry when I got home, but there wasn't anything to eat.

B · there has been / there have been (present perfect)

JAMES
Look! There's been an accident.
  • Look! There's been an accident. (there's been = there has been)
  • This road is very dangerous. There have been many accidents.

Compare there was (past):

  • There was an accident last night. (не There has been an accident last night.)

Past simple и present perfect — Unit 20.

C · there will be

DAVID
There will be rain tomorrow afternoon.
  • Do you think there will be a lot of people at the concert on Saturday?
  • Our boss is leaving, so there will be a new boss soon.
  • I'm going on holiday tomorrow. I'll pack my bag today because there won't be time tomorrow. (there won't be = there will not be)

Our town: then and now

Найдите there was/were, there has/have been и there will be.

Our town has changed a lot. Fifty years ago there was only one school, and there were no big shops. There wasn't a hospital — people had to go to the city.

In the last ten years there have been a lot of changes. There's been a new hospital, and there have been three new schools.

Last year there was a big festival in the main square, and there were thousands of visitors.

Next year there will be a new park by the river, and there will be a cinema too. But there won't be any more car parks in the centre.
