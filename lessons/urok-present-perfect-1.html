I have done (present perfect 1)

Действие в прошлом — результат сейчас

A
Presentation

Было → стало

Две серии картинок: действие произошло — и результат виден сейчас.

has cleaned / have gone — это present perfect (have + past participle)

positive / negative

I / we / you / theyhave ('ve)
have not (haven't)		cleaned / finished / started / lost / done / been / gone
he / she / ithas ('s)
has not (hasn't)		cleaned / finished / started / lost / done / been / gone

cleaned, finished… — это past participle (третья форма).

question

haveI / we / you / theycleaned? finished? started? lost? done? been? gone?
hashe / she / itcleaned? finished? … gone?

Правильные глаголы (regular)

clean → I have cleaned
finish → we have finished
start → she has started

Past participle на -ed — так же, как past simple.

Неправильные глаголы (irregular)

Иногда past simple и past participle совпадают:
buy → I bought / I have boughthave → he had / he has had
Иногда они разные:
break → I broke / I have brokensee → you saw / you have seen
fall → it fell / it has fallengo → they went / they have gone

Past participle не на -ed. Все три формы — в Словаре, вкладка «Неправильные глаголы».

Когда используем: действие в прошлом — результат сейчас

  • I've lost my keys. (= I can't find my keys now)
  • 'Where's Max?' — 'He's gone to the shop.' (= he is at the shop now)
  • We've bought a new sofa. (= we have a new sofa now)
  • It's Anna's birthday tomorrow and I haven't bought her a present. (= I don't have a present for her now)
  • 'Kate is away.' — 'Oh, where has she gone?' (= where is she now?)
  • Can I take this magazine? Have you finished with it? (= do you need it now?)

Is Kate ready?

Что Кейт уже сделала, а что — нет? Найдите все have/has + третья форма.

It's Saturday evening, and Kate is having a party at eight. It's seven o'clock now. Is she ready?

She has cleaned the flat and she has made a big cake. She has invited ten guests, and six people have already said yes.

But there are some problems. Kate has lost her phone, so she can't call anybody. Her brother has gone to the shop, but he hasn't come back yet.

And she hasn't bought any flowers! Oh well — the guests won't notice. The doorbell! Somebody has arrived early.

Sounds & Speech

've и 's — короткие формы

В речи have и has почти всегда сокращаются.

1 · I've /aɪv/, she's /ʃiːz/

I've lost my keys.
She's gone out.
We've finished.

2 · haven't /ˈhævnt/, hasn't /ˈhæznt/

I haven't finished.
He hasn't come back.

3 · Вопрос и краткий ответ

Have you finished? — Yes, I have.
Where has she gone?
