Sasha in London

Last year my friend Sasha moved to London because she wanted to learn English. When she got there, she stayed with a family who lived near the station.

The family she stayed with were very friendly, but the room she had was very small, so she looked for a flat.

Now she lives in a flat which is near the river. 'If I had more money, I'd live in the centre,' she says.

I'll visit her when I have a holiday. If I go in summer, we'll have a picnic in the park where she runs every morning.

Вопросы на слух

Слушайте и повторяйте. Обычная и медленная скорость помогают разобрать фразу.

1 · so / because / when

It was very hot, so I opened the window.
I'll call you when I get home.

2 · if

If I'm late, don't wait for me.
If I had the money, I'd buy a car.

3 · who / which

A thief is a person who steals things.
An airplane is a machine which flies.

4 · the hotel we stayed at

The hotel we stayed at was near the station.
These are the books I told you about.
