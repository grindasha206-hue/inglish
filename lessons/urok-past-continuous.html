I was doing (past continuous)

Что происходило в определённый момент прошлого

A
Presentation

Сейчас и в 4 часа

Картинка: сейчас 6 часов, Пол дома и смотрит телевизор. В 4 часа его не было дома — он был в спортклубе и плавал в бассейне.

now (6 o'clock)It is 6 o'clock now. Paul is at home. He is watching TV.
4 o'clockAt 4 o'clock he wasn't at home. He was at the sports club. He was swimming in the pool. He wasn't watching TV.

Шкала времени

3.30he started swimmingначал плавать
4.00he was swimmingв процессе
4.15he finished swimmingзакончил

Past continuous — действие было в процессе в определённый момент прошлого: начато раньше, закончено позже.

was / were + -ing — это past continuous

positive

I / he / she / itwasdoing / watching / playing / swimming / living…
we / you / theyweredoing / watching / playing / swimming / living…

negative

I / he / she / itwas not (wasn't)doing…
we / you / theywere not (weren't)doing…

question

wasI / he / she / itdoing? watching? playing?…
werewe / you / theydoing? watching? playing?…
  • What were you doing at 10.30 yesterday? Were you sleeping?
  • 'What did she say?' — 'Sorry, I wasn't listening.'
  • It was snowing, so we stayed at home.
  • In 2015 my parents were living in Moscow.
  • Today he's wearing jeans, but yesterday he was wearing a suit.
  • I woke up early yesterday. It was a lovely morning. The sun was shining and the birds were singing.

Правописание (spelling)

live → livingrun → runninglie → lying

am / is / are + -ing (present) → was / were + -ing (past)

present

I'm working (now).
It isn't raining (now).
What are you doing (now)?

past

I was working at 10.30 last night.
It wasn't raining when we went out.
What were you doing at 3 o'clock?

The power cut

Что делали люди в тот момент, когда погас свет? Найдите все was/were + -ing.

At eight o'clock last night there was a power cut in our street. Suddenly all the lights went out. What were people doing at that moment?

Mrs Green was cooking dinner in her kitchen. Her husband wasn't helping her — he was watching football on TV.

The students in flat 4 weren't studying. They were playing loud music, and their neighbour was knocking on the wall.

Little Sam was having a bath, and his parents were reading in the living room. And I was writing an email to my boss. I didn't finish it!

Sounds & Speech

was / were + -ing в потоке речи

Слушайте и повторяйте. Обычная и медленная скорость помогают разобрать фразу.

1 · Слабые was /wəz/ и were /wə/

I was working.
They were sleeping.
It was raining.

2 · Окончание -ing /ɪŋ/

В конце звучит носовое ŋ — без «г».

swimming
running
lying

3 · Вопрос и ответ

What were you doing at three o'clock?
Were you sleeping? — No, I wasn't.

Мой вчерашний день по часам

Напишите 6–8 предложений: что вы делали вчера в 8 утра, в 11, в 2 часа дня, в 6 и в 10 вечера. Используйте was/were + -ing.

  • At 8 o'clock I was… — having breakfast, going to work…
  • At 2 o'clock I wasn't… I was…
  • My family / friends were…
  • It was raining / the sun was shining…
0 слов
Пример: At 8 o'clock yesterday I was having breakfast. At 11 I was sitting at my desk and I was answering emails. At 2 o'clock I wasn't working — I was having lunch with a colleague. At 6 I was going home by bus. It was raining and people were running to the bus stop. At 10 o'clock I was reading a book in bed.
