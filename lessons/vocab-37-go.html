A · Go

go

Нажмите на слово — перевод и 🔊.

goидти, ехать means to move from one place to another. (wentпошёл (прош. от go) , goneушёл (3-я форма go) )

I go to work by bike. My brother goes by car. (go by bikeездить на велосипеде )

We went to Paris last summer.

Is this train going to Granada?

Shall we go to the swimming pool today?

Where does this road go?

B · Go + prepositions

go + предлоги

go intoвойти в
go out ofвыйти из
go upподниматься
go downспускаться

Kim went in(to) his room and shut the door. (go intoвойти в )

Yuko went out of the house and into the garden. (go out ofвыйти из )

Arthur was tired. He went up the stairs slowly. (go upподниматься )

The phone was ringing. She went down the stairs quickly. (go downспускаться )

C · Future plans

be going to

be going toсобираться is often used to talk about future plans.

Jan is going to study maths at university.

We're going to visit my aunt in New York soon.

I'm going to learn five new words every day.

D · Go + -ing for activities

go + -ing

Go is often used with -ing for different activities.

go shoppingходить за покупками
go swimmingходить плавать
go dancingходить на танцы
go sightseeingосматривать достопримечательности
go skiingкататься на лыжах
go fishingходить на рыбалку
Error warning
Let's go swimming and then go shopping. [NOT Let's go to / for swimming and then go to / for shopping.]
I go there every week. I don't want to go anywhere / somewhere else. [NOT I go to there every week. I don't want to go to anywhere / somewhere else.]
I must go home at 10 o'clock. [NOT I must go to / at home at 10 o'clock.]

Фразы: go homeидти домой , go thereходить туда , go somewhere elseпойти куда-то ещё .
