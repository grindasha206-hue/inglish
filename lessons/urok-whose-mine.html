Presentation

Whose is this? It's mine/yours/hers etc.

A · my → mine, our → ours …

mineoursyourshisherstheirs
I→my→mine
we→our→ours
you→your→yours
he→his→his
she→her→hers
they→their→theirs
It's my money.It's mine.
It's our money.It's ours.
It's your money.It's yours.
It's his money.It's his.
It's her money.It's hers.
It's their money.It's theirs.

B · my/your + noun и mine/yours без noun

my/your etc. + noun (my hands / your book etc.):

  • My hands are cold.
  • Is this your book?
  • Helen gave me her umbrella.
  • It's their problem, not our problem.

mine/yours etc. — без noun:

  • Is this book mine or yours? (= my book or your book)
  • I didn't have an umbrella, so Sarah gave me hers. (= her umbrella)
  • It's their problem, not ours. (= not our problem)
  • We went in our car, and they went in theirs. (= their car)

his — с noun и без:

  • 'Is this his camera or hers?' 'It's his.'

C · a friend of mine / a friend of his / some friends of yours etc.

  • I went out to meet a friend of mine. (не a friend of me)
  • Tom was in the restaurant with a friend of his. (не a friend of him)
  • Are those people friends of yours? (не friends of you)

D · Whose …?

  • Whose phone is this? (= Is it your phone? his phone? my phone? etc.)

whose — с noun и без:

Whose money is this?
Whose is this?		It's mine.
Whose shoes are these?
Whose are these?		They're John's.

Reading

After the party

Найдите все mine, yours, hers, theirs и Whose.

After the party there were a lot of things on the table. 'Whose phone is this?' asked Kate. 'It's mine,' said Tom.

'And whose keys are these?' 'They're not mine. I think they're Anna's.' 'No, they aren't hers. Hers are red.'

'Is this your bag or Lisa's?' 'It's hers. Mine is black.' 'And this umbrella? Is it yours?' 'No, it's theirs — Max and Olga's.'

In the end only one thing was left — a blue jacket. Whose was it? Nobody knew. Maybe it belonged to a friend of Tom's!
