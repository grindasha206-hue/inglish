a lot · much · many

much + неисчисл., many + исчисл., a lot of — с любыми

A
Presentation

a lot · much · many

A · much, many, a lot of

a lot of money
not much money
a lot of books
not many books

much + uncountable noun
(much food / much money etc.):

  • Did you buy much food?
  • We don't have much luggage.
  • How much money do you want?
  • A: Do you have any money?
    B: I have some, but not much.

many + plural noun
(many books / many people etc.):

  • Did you buy many books?
  • We don't know many people.
  • How many photos did you take?
  • A: Did you take any photos?
    B: I took some, but not many.

a lot of + both types of noun:

  • We bought a lot of food.
  • Paula doesn't have a lot of free time.
  • We bought a lot of books.
  • Did they ask a lot of questions?

We say:

  • There is a lot of food/money/water …
    (singular verb)
  • There are a lot of trees/shops/people … (plural verb)
  • A lot of people speak English. (не speaks)

B · much в вопросах и отрицаниях

much — в вопросах и отрицаниях:

  • Do you drink much coffee?
  • I don't drink much coffee.

Но в утвердительных предложениях much обычно не используем:

  • I drink a lot of coffee. (не I drink much coffee)
  • 'Do you drink much coffee?' 'Yes, a lot.' (не Yes, much)

many и a lot of — во всех типах предложений:

  • We have many friends / a lot of friends.
  • We don't have many friends / a lot of friends.
  • Do you have many friends / a lot of friends?

C · much и a lot без существительного

  • Amy spoke to me, but she didn't say much.
  • 'Do you watch TV much?' 'No, not much.' (= not often)
  • We like films, so we go to the cinema a lot. (не go to the cinema much)
  • I don't like him very much.

Books and films

I love reading, so I have a lot of books. But I don't have much space in my flat, so I can't buy many more.

How much time do I spend reading? A lot! I read every evening. I don't watch TV much.

My sister doesn't read many books. She prefers films and goes to the cinema a lot. She doesn't have much free time — she has three children!

There are a lot of good bookshops in my town. How many books did I buy last month? Not many — only two.
