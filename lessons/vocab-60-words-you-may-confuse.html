This unit looks at words which are easy to mix up.

quite /kwaɪt/ / quiet /ˈkwaɪət/

This book is quiteдовольно good. (bad → quite good → good)

My bedroom is very quietтихий . (= silent)

lose /luːz/ / loose /luːs/

A: Why do I always loseтерять my keys! B: Here they are. A: Oh, thank you!

If you lose something, you do not know where it is / you can't find it.

These trousers are very looseсвободный, широкий . (loose means they are not tight, because they are too big)

loseтерять
looseсвободный, широкий

fell / felt

fellупал (past of fall) is the past of fall. Yesterday I fell and broke my arm.

feltчувствовал (past of feel) is the past of feel. I felt ill yesterday, but I feel OK today.

cook / cooker

He is a very good cookповар . [the person who cooks]

This cookerплита costs £500. [the thing you cook on]

cookповар
cookerплита
B · Similar or related meanings

Похожие по значению

lend / borrow

If you lendодалживать (давать) something, you give it.

If you borrowбрать взаймы something, you get it.

Sam wants a bicycle:

SAM: Will you lend me your bicycle? (= you give it to me for one day / an hour, etc.)

or Can I borrow your bicycle? (= I get it from you)

RITA: Yes, take it. SAM: Thanks.

check / control

The passport officer checkedпроверил my passport. [looked at it]

We use the mouse to controlуправлять the computer. [tell it what to do]

Follow-up

lie / lay, rise / raise

Посмотрите в словаре и запишите разницу: lie – lay – lain / lay – laid – laid; rise – rose – risen / raise – raised – raised.

Проверить главное различие

lie – lay – lain: лежать; без дополнения: I lie on the sofa.

lay – laid – laid: класть что-то: I lay the book on the table.

rise – rose – risen: подниматься; без дополнения: The sun rises.

raise – raised – raised: поднимать что-то: Please raise your hand.
